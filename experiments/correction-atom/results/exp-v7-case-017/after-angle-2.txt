- Workflow orchestration latency was studied in heterogeneous GPU clusters across 140 jobs, with median overhead 18ms and 95th percentile 61ms.  
- Overhead scaled sub-linearly with job count, with 70% of overhead attributed to scheduler queue contention and the rest to checkpoint serialization.  
- Smith et al. (2024) confirmed these findings, noting that 90% of overhead arises from network stack overhead, contradicting the queue-contention theory.
