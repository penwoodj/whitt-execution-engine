- Small language models under 3B parameters can improve their outputs when paired with an external deterministic verifier, gaining an average of 18% accuracy on GSM8K.  
- The verifier provides binary feedback (pass/fail) on each candidate output, breaking the self-confirmation bias inherent in self-critique.  
- This improvement is consistent with Zhang et al. (2023), who found no measurable benefit from pure self-critique in small models.
