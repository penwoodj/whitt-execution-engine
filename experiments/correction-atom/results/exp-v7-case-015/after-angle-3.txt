- Small language models under 3B parameters show no measurable improvement through pure self-critique, consistent with Zhang et al. (2023), but gain an average of 18% accuracy on GSM8K when paired with an external deterministic verifier.  
- The external verifier provides binary feedback (pass/fail) on each candidate output, breaking the self-confirmation bias inherent in self-critique.  
- The study references Zhang et al. (2023) as foundational work on self-confirmation bias in LLMs.
