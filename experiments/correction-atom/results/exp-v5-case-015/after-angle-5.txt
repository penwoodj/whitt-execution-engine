- Chen et al. (2024) show that small models under 3B parameters can improve their outputs when paired with an external deterministic verifier, gaining an average of 18% accuracy on GSM8K.  
- The authors reference Zhang et al. (2023) as foundational work on self-confirmation bias in LLMs.  
- Their verifier provides binary feedback (pass/fail) on each candidate output, which helps mitigate self-confirmation bias.
