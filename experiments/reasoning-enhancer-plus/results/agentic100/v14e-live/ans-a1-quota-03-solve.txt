### Step 1: Understand the rules

We are given a messy morning scenario involving teams using TB (terabytes) of storage. Let’s extract and interpret each rule carefully.

#### Base capacity:
- Each team has a **base** of **100 TB**.
- The base **never rolls over** — meaning it stays fixed at 100 TB per team.

#### Flagged unused banks:
- Teams can flag unused bank allocations into the **next month**.
- These flagged banks are **exactly moved to next month**, and **expire if unused in that next month**.
- This means: if a team uses less than its base, it flags the unused portion (base - used) for next month.
- The flagged amount is **not available this month** — only the base + overflow grants capacity.

#### Overflow pool:
- There's an **overflow pool of 60 TB**.
- It grants **one-shot requests strictly in arrival order**, each in full, until empty.
- No splitting: if a request exceeds pool size, it is denied (but denial costs nothing and touches nothing).
- Grants are given only to teams in the queue, in arrival order.

#### Key points:
- Banks are per-team — no transfers between teams.
- Overflow pool grants **only** in arrival order, one at a time, full amount.
- Denials cost nothing and do not affect any other team or state.
- The overflow pool is **not shared** with base capacity; it's an additional source of allocation.

#### Arrival order queue:
This morning’s queue (in arrival order):
1. Cascade requested 35 TB
2. Borealis requested 20 TB
3. Atlas requested  10 TB

Note: The request is for **this month**, and the overflow pool will grant these in order, only if available.

#### What happens to unused banks?
- Each team flags its unused portion (base - used) into next month.
- If not used in next month, it expires — but we are not told about next month usage. So we just record that they flagged their unused portions for next month.

We are to compute:
- Each team’s **capacity** for the coming month = base + bank (flagged unused) + granted overflow
- Pool's remainder (what remains in overflow pool after grants)
- Denied list — teams whose requests were denied, listed alphabetically
