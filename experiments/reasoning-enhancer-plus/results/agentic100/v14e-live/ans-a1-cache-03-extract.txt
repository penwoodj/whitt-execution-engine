The cleaned JSON object:

```json
{"hits": 2, "misses": 1}
```

represents a count of successful (hit) and unsuccessful (miss) attempts. Specifically:

- **Hits**: 2 — Indicates that there were 2 successful matches or requests.
- **Misses**: 1 — Indicates that there was 1 failed match or request.

This data is typically used in caching, search systems, or performance monitoring to track how often items are found versus not found.
