### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a system with severity levels (sev1, sev2, sev3) and specific handling for each:

#### Sev1:
- Pages primary + secondary immediately (2 pager slots)
- **Every deploy is blocked** while any sev1 is open
- When resolved, the slots are freed back to pool

#### Sev2:
- During business hours (09:00–17:00): pages primary only (1 slot)
- Outside business hours: pages on-call rotation (1 slot)
- **If open longer than 240 minutes**, it is re-treated as sev1
    - The earlier single page is released
    - A fresh pair (i.e., 2 slots) is issued
- Sev3: never pages

#### Slot pool:
- Total pool of 6 slots
- Free when a page is resolved

#### Deploy blocking:
- While any sev1 is open → every deploy is blocked and counted
- So, each attempt to deploy during that time counts as a blocked deploy
