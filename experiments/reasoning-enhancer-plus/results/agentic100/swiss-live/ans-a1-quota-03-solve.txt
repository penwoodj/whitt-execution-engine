### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a system with:

- **Base capacity**: Each team starts with 100 TB base.
- **Banking behavior**:
  - Unused banks are **flagged for exactly next month**, and expire if unused in that next month.
  - Banks are **per-team**, no transfers between teams.
- **Overflow pool**:
  - Holds 60 TB total.
  - Grants one-shot requests **strictly in arrival order**, each in full, until the pool is empty.
  - No splitting of requests — must be granted fully or denied.
  - Denials cost nothing and touch nothing (i.e., no impact on base or bank).
- **No rolls over** for base capacity — once used, it's gone; cannot roll over to next month.
