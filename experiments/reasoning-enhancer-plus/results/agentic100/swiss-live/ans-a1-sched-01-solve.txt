**Step 1: Understand the rules**

We have three jobs:

- J1: takes 6 minutes, due at minute 10  
- J2: takes 3 minutes, due at minute 8  
- J3: takes 4 minutes, due at minute 12  

Rules:

1. **One worker** — only one job runs at a time.

2. **Mandatory 1-minute cooldown between jobs**  
   → After finishing a job, the worker must wait 1 minute before starting the next job.  
   → This cooldown does *not* pause the clock (i.e., time passes continuously).  
   → So if a job ends at time T, the next job can start only at time T + 1.

3. **Maximize deadlines met**  
   → We want to schedule jobs so that as many as possible meet their deadlines.
