**Step 1: Understand the rules**

We have three jobs:

- J1: takes 8 minutes, due at minute 16  
- J2: takes 2 minutes, due at minute 5  
- J3: takes 3 minutes, due at minute 9  

Rules:

1. **Mandatory 1-minute cooldown between jobs**  
   → After finishing a job, the worker must wait 1 minute before starting the next job.  
   → This cooldown does *not* pause the clock (i.e., time passes continuously).  
   → So if a job ends at time T, the next job starts at T + 1.

2. **Maximize deadlines met**  
   → We want to schedule jobs in an order that results in as many jobs as possible meeting their deadlines.

3. **Ties broken by earlier-submitted jobs first (J1 before J2 before J3)**  
   → This means if two orders have the same outcome, we prefer the one where earlier-submitted jobs run first.  
   → But note: all three jobs landed at once — so submission order is fixed
