**Step 1: Understand the rules**

We have a worker who performs jobs one at a time. The key rules:

1. **Cooldown Rule**:  
   There is a mandatory 1-minute cooldown between jobs.  
   → This means after finishing one job, the worker must wait 1 minute before starting the next job.  
   → The clock does *not* pause during this cooldown — it continues to tick.

2. **Maximize deadlines met**  
   Goal: Maximize how many jobs meet their deadlines.

3. **Tie-breaking rule**:  
   If two jobs have equal deadline performance (i.e., same deadline or same time of completion), the earlier-submitted job runs first.  
   → So, J1 before J2 before J3 in submission order.

4. **Rejection Rule**:  
   Any job that would miss its deadline by 2 minutes or more gets rejected outright and never starts.  
   → If a job misses by ≥2 minutes → rejected (does not start).  
   → If it misses by <2 minutes → still runs, but counts as "miss".
