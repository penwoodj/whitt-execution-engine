### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a set of rules governing how pages (alerts) are issued, slots are used, escalations occur, and deploys are blocked.

#### Rule Breakdown:

1. **Sev1**:
   - Pages primary + secondary immediately → **2 pager slots**
   - While any Sev1 is open: every deploy is **blocked** and counted
   - When a Sev1 is resolved, the slots are freed (returned to pool)

2. **Se-2**:
   - During business hours (09:00–17:00): pages primary only → **1 slot**
   - Outside business hours (before 09:00 or after 17:00): pages on-call rotation → **1 slot**
   - If a Sev2 is open for longer than 240 minutes:
     - It is re-treated as Sev1
     - The earlier single page is released (i.e., freed)
     - A fresh pair (primary + secondary) is issued → **2 slots**
