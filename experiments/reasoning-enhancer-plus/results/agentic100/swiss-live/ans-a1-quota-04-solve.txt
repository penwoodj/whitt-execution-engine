### Step 1: Understand all rules

We are given a set of rules that govern how TB (terabytes) are allocated to teams and how overflow works.

#### Rule 1: "100 TB base each"
- Every team starts with a **base capacity** of **100 TB**.
- This is fixed. No rolling over or changing.

#### Rule 2: "flagged unused banks into exactly next month then expires if unused"
- Teams can flag unused bank allocations (i.e., unused TB from their base) to be moved to the **next month**.
- These flagged amounts become available in the **next month**, and if not used, they expire.
- This means: any unused portion of a team's base is stored as a "bank" for next month — but only if it’s actually flagged.

> Important: The rule says “flagged unused banks” are moved to next month. So:
> - If a team uses less than 100 TB, the **unused** amount (100 - used) is flagged.
> - That flagged amount becomes available in the **next month
