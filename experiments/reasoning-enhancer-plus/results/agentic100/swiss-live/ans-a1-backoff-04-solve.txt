**Task Summary (Rules):**

1. **Retry client doubles backoff from 2 seconds**:  
   - Initial backoff = 2 seconds  
   - Each retry, backoff is *doubled*  
   - So:  
     - Attempt 1 → 2 sec  
     - Attempt 2 → 4 sec  
     - Attempt 3 → 8 sec  
     - Attempt 4 → 16 sec  
     - Attempt -5 → 32 sec  
     - etc.

2. **Caps any single delay at 30 seconds**:  
   - If backoff would exceed 30 seconds, it is capped at 30 seconds.  
   - So:  
     - Any value > 30 becomes 30

3. **Gives up after 8 attempts**:  
   - Maximum number of attempts = 8  
   - The run succeeded on attempt 7 → so we go from attempt 1 to attempt 7 (inclusive)
