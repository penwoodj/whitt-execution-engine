**Step 1: Understand the rules**

We have a system with:

- A **residency cap**: 8 GB. This means at any time, the total resident memory (i.e., loaded models) must not exceed 8 GB.
- Jobs run in strict order: A → B → C → D
- Each job requires a model of specific size:
  - Job A: needs 5 GB model
  - Job B: needs 3 GB model
  - Job C: needs 5 GB model
  - Job D: needs 3 GB model
- Both models begin **loaded** at the start (before any job runs).
- After each job, its model is **unloaded** for maintenance.
- Each **load** costs 30 seconds.
- Each **unload** costs 10 seconds.
- The dashboard service does not change anything — it just observes.

We are to compute:
- Total number of **loads**
- Total time spent on **loading (in seconds)**
- Total time spent on **unloading (in seconds)**

Note: "loads" refers to how many times a model
