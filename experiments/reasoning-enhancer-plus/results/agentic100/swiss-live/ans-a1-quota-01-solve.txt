### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a system with:

- **Base capacity**: Each team starts with **100 TB** base. This never rolls over.
- **Flagged unused banks**: Teams can flag unused portions of their bank (i.e., unused TB) to be moved into the next month's pool.
  - Flagged amount is **moved exactly to the next month**, and expires if not used in that month.
- **Banks are per-team, no transfers** → each team manages its own bank independently.
- **Overflow pool**: Holds up to **50 TB**. It grants one-shot requests strictly in arrival order (FIFO), full amounts, no splitting.
  - Grants only until empty.
  - Denials cost nothing and touch nothing — so we just note them.
- **No splitting of requests** → each request is either fully granted or denied.
