### Step 1: Understand the rules

We are given:

- **TTL is exactly 90 key** (900 seconds) — this means a value remains valid for 900 seconds from the time it was written.
- **No refresh on read**: This means that when you read, the TTL does not get extended. The value stays valid only until its original TTL expires.
- **Flush invalidates everything present at the moment it runs**:
  - When a flush occurs, all existing keys are invalidated (i.e., removed from cache).
  - After flush, any new writes happen *after* the flush and will have their own TTL starting from that time.

- The dashboard service continuously renders this data but does **not change anything** — it just reads what is available at each moment.

We need to count:
- **Hits**: when a key exists and is valid (i.e., within TTL) at the read time.
- **Misses**: when a key does not exist or has expired at the read time.
