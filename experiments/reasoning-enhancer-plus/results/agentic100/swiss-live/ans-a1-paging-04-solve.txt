### Step 1: Understand all rules

We have a reconciliation time policy with severity levels (sev1, sev2, sev3) and specific behaviors:

#### Rule 1: Sev1 pages
- Primary + secondary immediately → **2 pager slots**
- This happens in **2 pager slots** (i.e., two slots used)
- While any sev1 is open → every deploy is blocked and counted

#### Rule 2: Sev2 pages
- In business hours (09:00–17:00): only primary, **1 slot**
- Outside business hours: on-call rotation, **1 slot**
- If a sev2 issue lasts longer than 240 minutes → re-treated as sev1:
  - The earlier single page is released
  - A fresh pair (i.e., two slots) is issued
- Note: "re-treated as sev1" implies it becomes sev1, so triggers the full sev1 rules

#### Rule .3: Sev3 pages
- Never pages → **0 slots used**
