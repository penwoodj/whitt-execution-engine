**Step 1: Understand the structure of the reconciliation process**

- There are **8 stages**, each taking **2 minutes**.
- After every stage, there is a **checkpoint**.
- If a run crashes, you must **replay from the last checkpoint**.
- Wasted partial minutes still burn wall clock (i.e., time spent in a failed stage counts toward total wall time).
- The dashboard service does not manage this process and renders all of this continuously — it doesn't change anything.
