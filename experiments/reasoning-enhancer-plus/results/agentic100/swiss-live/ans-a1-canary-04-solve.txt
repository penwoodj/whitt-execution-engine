Let's go step by step through every rule and the measured data to determine the correct output.

---

**Step 1: Understand the rollout steps**

The rollout happens in stages:

- Step 5%  
- Step 25%  
- Step 50%  
- Step 100%

Each stage is a percentage of traffic that is rolled out.

---

**Step 2: Rule for when a step is considered "completed"**

> "pauses moment any step measures error at or above 1.0 percent - completed step is one that finished clean, paused step parks traffic but doesn't count as done"

So:

- If the measured error rate **at or above 1.0%**, then:
  - The step is **paused** (traffic stops)
  - That step is **not counted as "done"**
- If the error rate is **below 1.0%**, then:
  - The step is considered **completed clean**

Note: Only steps that finish cleanly are counted in "stages_done".
