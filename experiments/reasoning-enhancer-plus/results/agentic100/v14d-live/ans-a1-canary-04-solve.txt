Let's go step by step through every rule and the measured data to determine the correct output.

---

**Step 1: Understand the rollout steps**

The rollout happens in stages:

- Step 5%  
- Step 25%  
- Step 50%  
- Step 100%

Each stage is a percentage of traffic that is rolled out.

---

**Step 2: Understand the rules for completion**

> "pauses moment any step measures error at or above 1.0 percent"

This means:
- If at any stage, the measured error rate reaches **1.0% or more**, then the rollout **pauses** at that step.
- The step is **not completed** (does not count as done).
- Only steps that finish clean (i.e., with error < 1.0%) are counted as "done".

> "completed step is one that finished clean, paused step parks traffic but doesn't count as done"

So:
- A stage is considered "done" only if it passed cleanly (error < 1.0%).
- If a stage fails (error ≥  1.0%), the rollout pauses at that stage and that stage does **not** count toward "stages_done".

> "rollout steps 5, 25, 50, then 100 percent of traffic"

- 5%
- 25%
- 50%
- 100%
