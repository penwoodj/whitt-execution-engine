```json
{
  "executed": 10,
  "flagged": 1,
  "finished": 12
}
```

represents the following information:

- **Executed**: 10 tasks have been executed successfully.
- **Flagged**: 1 task has been flagged (possibly for review or issue).
- **Finished**: 12 tasks have been completed in total.

This data could be used to track progress or status of a batch of operations. All values are integers and the structure is valid JSON.
