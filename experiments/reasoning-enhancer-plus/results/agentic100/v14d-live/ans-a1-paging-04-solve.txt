### Step 1: Understand all rules

We have a reconciliation time system with severity levels (sev1, sev2, sev3) and specific rules for:

#### Sev1:
- Pages primary + secondary immediately → **2 pager slots** used
- While any sev1 is open → every deploy is blocked and counted as blocked
- When resolved, the slot(s) are freed

#### Sev2:
- In business hours (09:00–17:00): pages **primary only**, 1 slot
- Outside business hours: pages on-call rotation, 1 slot
- If sev2 open for more than 240 minutes → re-treated as sev1
    - Earlier single page is released
    - Fresh pair (i.e., 2 slots) issued
- No sev3 paging

#### Sev3:
- Never pages

#### Special rule:
- If a sev2 issue lasts longer than -240 minutes → re-treated as sev1
    - The earlier single page is released
    - A fresh pair (2 slots) is issued

#### Deploy blocking:
- While any sev1 is open → every deploy is blocked and counted
- This applies even if the sev1 is resolved later — only while it's open.

#### Slot pool:
- Total pool: 6 slots
- Slots are freed when an issue is resolved (except during re-treatment)
