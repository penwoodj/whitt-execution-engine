### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a system with severity levels (sev1, sev2, sev3) and specific handling rules for each:

#### Sev1:
- Pages primary + secondary immediately → **2 pager slots** used.
- Every deploy is blocked while any sev1 is open → **each deploy attempt during sev1 open is blocked**.
- When resolved, the slots are freed (pool replenished).
- Opened at 09:00, resolved at 09:40 → duration = 40 minutes.

#### Sev2:
- During business hours (09:00–17:00): pages **primary only** → **1 slot**
- Outside business hours: pages on-call rotation → **1 slot**
- If open for more than 240 minutes, it is re-treated as sev1.
    - Then: the earlier single page (for that sev2) is released and a fresh pair (i.e., 2 slots) is issued.
- Sev3: never pages → no slots used.

#### General:
- Slots come from a pool of six. Free when resolved.
- While any sev1 is open, **every deploy is blocked** and counted as blocked.
- "Deploys attempted" are given: 09:10 and 16:0. We need to count how many were blocked.

#### Escalations:
- "Escalations" likely refers to cases where a sev2 that exceeds 240 minutes is re-treated as sev1 → this triggers an escalation.
- So, if any sev2 open for >240 minutes → it becomes sev1 and triggers escalation.
