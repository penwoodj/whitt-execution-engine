### Step 1: Understand the structure of the task

We have a reconciliation process with:

- **8 stages**, each taking **2 minutes**.
- After every stage, there is a **checkpoint**.
- If a crash occurs during execution, we must **replay from the last checkpoint**.
- Wasted partial minutes still count in wall clock time (i.e., even if a stage is only partially run, the time spent is included).
- The dashboard service does not manage this process and renders all of this continuously — it doesn't change anything.

We are told:

> Stages 1..5 committed → then crashed **2 minutes into stage 6**  
> Then: replayed stage 6 from scratch and ran the rest clean

Also:

> Total wall minutes across both runs and stages re-execute

This means we must compute total actual wall clock time (in minutes) spent during:
- The first run (up to crash)
- The second run (replay of stage 6, then completion of remaining stages)

And determine how many stages were rerun.

### Step 2: Break down the timeline

- Then, it crashes **2 minutes into stage 6**.

→ Stage 6 is not completed. The crash happens at 2 minutes in.

Total wall time for first run =  
10 minutes (stages 1–5) + 2 minutes (first part of stage 6) = **12 minutes**
