### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a system with:

- **Base capacity**: Each team starts with 100 TB base (never rolls over).
- **Banking mechanism**:
  - Unused banks are **flagged into exactly next month**, and expire if unused.
  - Banks are **per-team**, no transfers between teams.
- **Overflow pool**:
  - Holds 60 TB total.
  - Grants one-shot requests in **arrival order**, full amount, no splitting.
  - Only grants until the pool is empty.
  - Denials cost nothing and touch nothing (i.e., no impact on any other state).
- **Compliance scanner**: Does not manage timestamps or change outcomes — we must compute based on facts.

We are to compute:
- Each team’s capacity for coming month: base + bank + granted overflow
- Pool remainder after all requests
- Denied list (teams that requested but were denied, in alphabetical order)
