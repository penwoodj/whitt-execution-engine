### Step-by-step analysis:

#### Rule: TTL = 300 seconds
- A key is valid from the time it's written until (written_time + 300), unless invalidated by flush.
- So, a key written at time T is valid until T + 300, **unless** it was flushed earlier.

#### Flush happens at second 200:
- At second 200, all keys that were present *at that moment* are invalidated.
- "Present" means: the key was written before or at second 200 and hasn't expired yet (i.e., written_time + 300 > 200).
