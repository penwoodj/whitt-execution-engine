### Step 1: Understand the rules

We have a set of rules governing how pages (alerts) are issued, slots are used, and deploys are blocked.

#### Rule 1: Sev1 pages
- Primary + secondary immediately → **2 pager slots**
- Every deploy is **blocked** while any sev1 is open.
- Counted as a deploy block.

#### Rule 2: Sev2 pages
- During business hours (09:00–17:00): page primary only → **1 slot**
- Outside business hours (before 09:00 or after 09:00–17:00): page on-call rotation → **1 slot**
- If sev2 is open for longer than 240 minutes, it is re-treated as sev1:
  - The earlier single page is released (i.e., freed from slot)
  - A fresh pair (primary + secondary) is issued → **2 slots**
- Sev3: never pages

#### Rule 3: Slot management
- Total pool of slots = 6
- Slots are freed when a page is resolved.
- While any sev1 is open, all deploys are blocked.

#### Rule 4: Deploy blocking
- Any time there's an active sev1, **all deploys are blocked** and counted as "deploys_blocked".
