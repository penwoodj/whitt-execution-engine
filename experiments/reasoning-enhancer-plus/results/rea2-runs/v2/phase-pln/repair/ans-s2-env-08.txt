Here's the breakdown of the job scheduling and the resulting JSON output:

**1. Scheduling with Strict Priority:**

*   **C (3 min):** Runs first due to strict priority.
*   **A (3 min):** Runs second, after C.
*   **B (8 min):** Runs last, after A.

**2. Calculating Makespan (T):**

The makespan is the total time taken for all jobs to complete.
*   C completes at 3 minutes.
*   A completes at 3 + 3 = 6 minutes.
*   B completes at 6 + 8 = 14 minutes.
Therefore, T = 14 minutes.

**3. Calculating Average Wait Time (W):**

*   **C:** Waits 0 minutes (no jobs before it).
*   **A:** Waits 3 minutes (C runs first).
*   **B:** Waits 6 + 3 = 9 minutes (C and A run first).

Total wait time = 0 + 3 + 9 = 12 minutes.
Average wait time (W) = Total wait time / Number of jobs = 12 / 3 = 4 minutes.

**4. Constructing the JSON:**

```json
{"order": ["C", "A", "B"], "completion_min": 14, "idle_min": 0, "avg_wait_min": 4}
```
