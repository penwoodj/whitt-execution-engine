**Step 1: Understand the token budget**

- Total token budget = 100,000 tokens

**Step 2: Understand routing rules**

There are three models:

- **Model A**: Routes every document to Model A at **2k tokens per doc**
- **Model B**: Promotes exactly the failures of Model A to Model B at **5k tokens per doc**
- **Model C**: Reserved only for documents flagged as **legal**, at **9k tokens per doc**

Additional constraints:

- The legal docs never overlap with A's failures → so no doc is both a failure of A and legal.
- If the plan would exceed budget, overflow docs are dropped back to 2k each (i.e., they go to Model A at 2k instead of higher cost).
- We do **not** manage reads spend — we only care about routing decisions and token spend.
- We **never reroute** once a decision is made.
