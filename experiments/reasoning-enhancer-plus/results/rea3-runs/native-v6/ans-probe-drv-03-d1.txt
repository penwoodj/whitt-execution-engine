To find the final price, we will apply the percentage changes sequentially to the initial service price of 84.

**Step 1: Calculate the increase.**
The initial price is 84. It rises by 25%.
Increase amount = $84 \times 0.25$
$84 \times 0.25 = 21$

New price after increase = Initial price + Increase amount
New price = $84 + 21 = 105$

**Step 2: Calculate the decrease.**
The new price is 105. It drops by 20%.
Decrease amount = $105 \times 0.20$
$105 \times 0.20 = 21$

Final price = New price - Decrease amount
Final price = $105 - 21 = 84$

**Verification:**
Alternatively, we can use multipliers:
Initial Price $\times (1 + 0.25) \times (1 - 0.20)$
$84 \times 1.25 \times 0.80$
$84 \times 1 = 84$

The final price is 84.
