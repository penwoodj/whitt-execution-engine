To solve this problem:

- Jobs: A (3 min), B (8 min), C (3 min)
- Worker has strict priority: C runs before A, A runs before B
- The order is: **C**, **A**, **B**
- Makespan (T) is the total time taken to complete all jobs.
- Average wait time (W) is the average of the waiting times for each job before they start.

### Step 1: Determine the makespan (T)
The sequence is:
- C (3 min) starts at 0, finishes at 3
- A (3 min) starts at 3, finishes at 6
- B (8 min) starts at 6, finishes at 14

Makespan = **14 minutes**

### Step 2: Determine the average wait time (W)
Wait times before starting:
- C: 0 (starts immediately)
- A: 3 (waited for C to finish)
- B: 6 (waited for C and A to finish)

Average wait time:
$$
W = \frac{0 + 3 + 6}{3} = \frac{9}{3} = 3
