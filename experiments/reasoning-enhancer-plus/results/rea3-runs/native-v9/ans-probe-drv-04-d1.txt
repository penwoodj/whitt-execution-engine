To solve this problem, we need to determine how many minutes it takes for 7 machines to complete a total of 140 tasks, given that each machine handles 5 tasks per minute.

**Step 1: Calculate the total processing rate of the fleet.**
Each machine processes 5 tasks per minute.
There are 7 machines.
Total tasks processed per minute = (Number of machines) × (Tasks per machine per minute)
Total tasks per minute = $7 \times 5 = 35$ tasks/minute.

**Step 2: Calculate the time required to complete the total job.**
The total number of tasks needed is 140.
The processing rate is 35 tasks per minute.
Time (in minutes) = (Total tasks) / (Tasks per minute)
Time = $140 / 35$

**Step 3: Perform the division.**
$140 \div 35 = 4$

So, it takes 4 minutes to complete the job.

4 minutes
