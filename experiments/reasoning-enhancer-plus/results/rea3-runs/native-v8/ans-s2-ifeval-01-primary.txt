- auth: Manages user authentication and authorization  
- billing: Handles invoice generation and payment processing  
- cache: Stores frequently accessed data to reduce latency  
- search: Enables fast retrieval of relevant records from datasets  
- indexer: Builds and maintains searchable indexes from source data  
- gateway: Routes requests to appropriate backend services  
- queue: Processes asynchronous tasks in a prioritized order  
- audit: Logs and tracks system events for compliance and debugging
