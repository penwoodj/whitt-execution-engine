To determine how many minutes it will take for 7 machines to complete a job that requires 140 tasks, we start by calculating the total number of tasks processed per minute:

- Each machine handles 5 tasks per minute.
- Total tasks per minute = 7 machines × 5 tasks/minute = 35 tasks/minute.

Next, we calculate how many minutes are needed to complete 140 tasks:

- Time required = Total tasks / Tasks per minute = 140 / 35 = 4 minutes.

Answer: 4 minutes.
