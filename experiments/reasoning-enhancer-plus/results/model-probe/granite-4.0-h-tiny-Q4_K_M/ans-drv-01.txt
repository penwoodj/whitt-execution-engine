To find the number of events per queue, first calculate the total number of unique events:

Total groups = 17
Events per group = 24

Total unique events = 17 × 24 = 408

Then subtract the duplicates:
Unique events after removing duplicates = 408 - 148 = 260

Finally, divide evenly across the 4 queues:
Events per queue = 260 ÷ 4 = 65

Therefore, there are 65 events in each queue.
