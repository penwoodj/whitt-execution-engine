 The total number of tasks required to complete one job is 140. Each minute, seven machines can handle five tasks each, resulting in a total of 35 tasks completed per minute across the fleet. To find out how many minutes it will take to complete the job, we need to divide the total number of tasks by the number of tasks completed per minute:

Minutes until done = Total tasks / Tasks per minute
= 140 tasks / 35 tasks per minute
≈ 4 minutes.

So, it would take approximately 4 minutes to complete the job.
