To find the final price after a rise of 25% followed by a drop of 20%, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the increase in price due to the 25% rise:
   84 * (1 + 0.25) = 84 * 1.25 = 105

2. Then, calculate the decrease in price due to the 20% drop from the new price of $105:
   105 * (1 - 0.20) = 105 * 0.80 = 84

So, after a rise of 25% and then a drop of 20%, the final price remains at:

\boxed{84}
