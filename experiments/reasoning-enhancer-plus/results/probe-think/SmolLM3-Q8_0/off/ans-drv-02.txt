To find the total length of the conduit, we need to add up all its segments:

1. The first segment is 2.5 km.
2. The second segment is 3800 m, which needs to be converted into kilometers (since the other lengths are in kilometers). There are 1000 meters in a kilometer, so:
   \[
   3800 \text{ m} = \frac{3800}{1000} \text{ km} = 3.8 \text{ km}
   \]
3. The third segment is back 0.7 km.

Now we add all the lengths together:

\[
2.5 \text{ km} + 3.8 \text{ km} + 0.7 \text{ km} = (2.5 + 3.8 + 0.7) \text{ km}
\]

Adding these values gives us:
\[
2.5 + 3.8 = 6.3
\]
\[
6.3 + 0.7 = 7.0
\]

So, the total length of the conduit is:

\[
7.0 \text{ km}
\]
