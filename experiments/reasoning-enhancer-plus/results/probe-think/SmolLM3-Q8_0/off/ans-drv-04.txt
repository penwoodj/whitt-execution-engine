To determine how long it will take to complete a job that requires 140 tasks, we first need to calculate the rate at which the machines can process tasks. 

Each machine handles 5 tasks per minute, and there are 7 machines in total. Therefore, the combined processing capacity of all machines is:
\[ 7 \text{ machines} \times 5 \text{ tasks/machine/minute} = 35 \text{ tasks/minute} \]

The job requires a total of 140 tasks to be processed. To find out how many minutes it will take for the fleet to complete this task, we divide the total number of tasks by the processing rate:
\[ \frac{140 \text{ tasks}}{35 \text{ tasks/minute}} = 4 \text{ minutes} \]

So, it will take 4 minutes until the job is done.
