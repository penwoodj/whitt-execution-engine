The problem states that there are 7 machines, each handling 5 tasks per minute. This means the total processing rate is 7 machines × 5 tasks/minute = 35 tasks per minute. One batch of 35 tasks completes at this rate. A job requires 140 tasks total. Since one batch (35 tasks) completes every minute, to process 140 tasks, it will take 140 / 35 = 4 batches. Therefore, the time required is 4 minutes.

Answer: 4
