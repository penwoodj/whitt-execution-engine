1. Deploy new version to staging with 10% real user traffic in canary mode.
2. Monitor error rate, latency, and key metrics for at least 5 minutes during canary phase.
3. If error rate exceeds 2%, initiate rollback to previous stable version immediately.
4. After confirming stability, incrementally increase canary traffic to 50% over the next 10 minutes.
5. Once fully live with no issues, promote to production and remove staging deployment.
