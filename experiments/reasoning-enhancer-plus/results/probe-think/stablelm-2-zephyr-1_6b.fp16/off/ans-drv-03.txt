To find the final price after both events occur, we need to calculate the initial price (before any percentage change) and apply the subsequent percentage increase and decrease.

Initial Price: Let's assume the initial price is 100 for this example.
Percentage Increase: The service price increases by 20% or 0.20. To find the new price after the increase, we multiply the initial price by (1 + Percentage Increase):
New Price = Initial Price * (1 + Percentage Increase)
= 100 * (1 + 0.20)
= 100 * 1.20
= 120

Percentage Decrease: The new price drops by 30% or 0.30. To find the final price after this decrease, we multiply the new price by (1 - Percentage Decrease):
Final Price = New Price * (1 - Percentage Decrease)
= 120 * (1 - 0.30)
= 120 * 0.70
= 84

Therefore, the final price after both events occur is 84.
