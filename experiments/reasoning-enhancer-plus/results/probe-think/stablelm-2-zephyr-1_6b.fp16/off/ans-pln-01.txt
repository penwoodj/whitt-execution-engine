Here's a 5-step numbered deploy runbook with a canary phase, rollback trigger, and error rate threshold:

1. Canary Phase (0% Traffic):
	* Set up a canary environment for testing the deployment.
	* Configure the canary environment to send traffic to a specific percentage of servers.
2. Rollback Trigger (Error Rate Exceeds 2%):
	* Monitor the canary environment's performance and error rate.
	* If the error rate exceeds 2%, immediately roll back the deployment to the previous version.
3. Deployment Phase (100% Traffic):
	* Deploy the new feature or update on all servers.
4. Testing & Validation:
	* Conduct thorough testing of the deployed feature or update, ensuring it meets business requirements and is stable.
5. Rollback Trigger (Error Rate Exceeds 2%):
	* If any issues are discovered during testing, immediately roll back to the previous version.

Note: This runbook assumes a standard deployment process with automated tools for rolling back in case of errors. Adjust as needed based on your specific environment and rollback procedures.
