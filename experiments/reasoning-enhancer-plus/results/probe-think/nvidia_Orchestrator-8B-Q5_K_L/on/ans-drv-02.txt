To find the total length of the conduit, we add all the distances together:

- First segment: **2.5 km**
- Second segment: **3800 m = 3.8 km** (since 1 km = 1000 m)
- Third segment: **0.7 km**

Now sum them up:

$$
2.5 \, \text{km} + 3.8 \, \text{km} + 0.7 \, \text{km} = 7.0 \, \text{km}
$$
