To determine how long it takes to complete a job that requires **140 tasks**, given that **7 machines each handle 5 tasks per minute** (so **35 tasks per minute total**), we can calculate:

$$
\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total Tasks}}{\text{Tasks Per Minute}} = \frac{140}{35} = 4 \text{ minutes}
$$

**Answer: 4 minutes**
