4.  **Post-Rollback Limit Rule**: Applies the tightened limit of `0.8` to every subsequent step after a rollback occurs.

5.  **Second Breach/Stop Rule**: If observed rate > Current Limit (which is now `0.8`) on any step following a rollback, stops the climb immediately with status STOPPED.
