### Phase 1: Rule Scan
The following rules govern the state changes during the event list (LOW1 arrival/grant, LOW2 arrival/grant, HIGH arrival/preempt):

1.  **Arrival/Grant Rule**: Jobs arrive in order of their start minutes; a job starting at minute $s$ banks a checkpoint at absolute minute $s+2$.
2.  **Resume Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW outranks a fresh LOW at any tie (start/resume time).
3.  **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrives and instantly preempts the running LOW; minutes past the last bank are wasted.
4.  **Core Continuity Rule**: The core never idles while a LOW waits; the next waiting LOW starts in the exact minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
5.  **Completion Order Rule**: LOW jobs are listed by finish minute (HIGH excluded from list).

**Trigger Mapping:**
*   `LOW1` arrival triggers Grant/Start at $t=0$.
*   `LOW2` arrival triggers Grant/Wait at $t=3$ (arrives while core busy).
*   `HIGH` arrival triggers Preemption at $t=3$, followed by Resume of waiting jobs.
