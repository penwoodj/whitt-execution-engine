### Phase 1 Rule Scan
The following rules govern the state changes during this night's events:
1. **Arrival Rule**: LOW jobs arrive at their specified start minutes; they wait if the core is busy or no job is running.
2. **Start/Resume Rule**: A waiting LOW job starts immediately when the core becomes free (preempted finished, or idle). Resumed jobs outrank fresh ones at ties.
3. **Checkpoint Bank Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` and every subsequent even running minutes (absolute time). Banks landing exactly on HIGH's arrival count as valid.
4. **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrives; whatever LOW is currently running is preempted immediately. Work past the last bank is wasted.
5. **Waste Calculation Rule**: Wasted minutes = `total_duration - banked_minutes` (where `banked_minutes` are even integers $\le$ `preemption_time`).
6. **Core Continuity Rule**: The core never idles; as soon as a LOW finishes or is preempted, the next waiting LOW starts in that same minute.
7. **Completion Order Rule**: LOW jobs are listed by their absolute finish minutes (lowest first).

**Trigger Mapping**:
- `LOW1` Arrival: Minute 0.
- `LOW2` Arrival: Minute 3.
- `HIGH` Arrival: Minute 5 (Triggers Preemption of running LOW).
- `LOW1` Expiry/Return: Minute 7 (Implicitly handled by waste calculation at preemption).
