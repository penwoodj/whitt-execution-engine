### 1. Proven Capabilities of Small-Scale Agentic Systems
Research confirms that models under 10 billion parameters can successfully function as autonomous agents when equipped with specific architectural enhancements and external tools:

*   **Tool Use and Function Calling:** Small models excel at "function calling," where they act as a router to execute pre-defined code snippets, API calls, or database queries. They do not need to generate the entire solution internally; instead, they orchestrate existing capabilities (e.g., Python execution via Sandboxed environments like `langchain` or `firecracker`).
*   **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** By integrating with external knowledge bases, small models overcome their limited internal parameter memory. They can perform complex research tasks by retrieving relevant documents and synthesizing answers based on that retrieved context rather than relying solely on pre-trained weights.
