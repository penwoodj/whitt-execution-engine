# Queue Lag Report: Final Deliverables

## 1. Workspace Layout & Methodology

**Location:** `/tmp/opencode/queue-run` (Synthetic Production Environment)

**Structure Decision:**
*   **Root:** `rules.md`, `decisions.md`, `readme.md`.
*   **Scripts:** `scripts/calc_lag.sh`, `scripts/correlate_rebalance.sh`, `scripts/verify_independent.sh`.
*   **Outputs:** `outputs/lag.json`, `outputs/lag-report.md`.
*   **Logs:** `logs/stage_1.log`, `logs/stage_2.log`.

**Methodology Note:**
The report is generated by parsing synthetic input data representing a message queue with 4 consumers, partitions, and lag snapshots. The analysis focuses on Consumer `worker-2` which exhibits bursty lag. Correlation logic matches lag spikes against three specific rebalance timestamps (`10:04:00`, `11:20:00`, `13:15:00`). Throughput is estimated via offset deltas (messages/minute). Missing data in the 20-minute window and backward offsets are surfaced as artifacts rather than smoothed. All calculations are precise (no rounding) to ensure traceability back to input rows.

## 3. Correlation with Rebalance Events (`outputs/lag-report.md`)

**Verdict:** Consumer `worker-2` is **Stuck due to Rebalance on P0**, exacerbated by a Hot Partition condition. The lag spikes correlate directly with the first two rebalance events, while the third event shows no significant impact on P0 lag.

### Event Correlation Table

| Rebalance ID | Timestamp | Lag Spike Confirmed? | Verdict / Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R1** | `10:04:00` | **Yes** | **Stuck due to Rebalance.** Consumer group split caused P0 processing pause. Offset delta dropped from 45 msg/min to 2.1 msg/min immediately post-event. |
| **R3** | `11:20:00` | **Yes** | **Hot Partition P0.** Rebalance completed, but P0 log end offset grew faster than consumer rate (Log Growth: +800 msgs vs Consumer: +450 msgs). Lag increased by 350 msgs. |
| **R_unknown**| `13:15:00` | No | Minor fluctuation (<10% of mean lag). Not correlated to major spike. |

**Anomaly Handling:**
*   *Missing Data:* The 20-minute window (`T+45min` to `T+65min`) contains no snapshots. This is recorded as a gap in the input stream, not an error in calculation.
*   *Backward Offsets:* Two instances where consumer offset decreased were flagged and excluded from throughput delta calculations to prevent negative rate artifacts.

---

## 4. Rules & Decisions (`rules.md`, `decisions.md`)

**Rules Invented:**
1.  **Burst Window Definition:** Average lag calculated over the 5 snapshots immediately following a spike >2x Mean Lag.
2.  **Throughput Denominator:** Must be positive offset delta; if negative, stage is marked "Offset Rollback" and excluded from rate calc.
3.  **Precision Rule:** No rounding of intermediate values; final output retains full integer precision to allow traceability.

**Decisions Log:**
*   *Mess Type:* Missing Snapshots (20-min window). **Action:** Documented in `lag.json` as gap, not interpolated.
*   *Mess Type:* Backward Offsets (2 instances). **Action:** Flagged as "Offset Rollback", excluded from throughput delta.
