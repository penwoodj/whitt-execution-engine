# Alert Storm Deduplication Pipeline Report

## Executive Summary
This analysis pipeline processed a raw alert stream containing **2000 alerts** fired within a single hour. The system successfully normalized timestamps, corrected ordering issues, grouped alerts into distinct incidents based on template and time proximity, identified "noise maker" templates, and generated a final deduplicated report. All artifacts are available in the workspace `/tmp/opencode/alert-run`.

## 2. Rules & Methodology (`rules.md`)
The following rules were explicitly defined to handle the messy input:

1.  **Noise Maker Definition**: A template is classified as a "noise maker" if it generates alerts more than **10 times** within the observation window. This threshold was chosen because single-incident fan-out rarely exceeds this count without indicating a systemic issue or data duplication error.
2.  **Timestamp Normalization**: Mixed timestamp formats (ISO 8601 and Unix epoch) are converted to ISO 8601 UTC for uniformity.
3.  **Ordering Correction**: Alerts with timestamps out of order relative to their sequence index are flagged as `quarantined_invalid_ts` rather than dropped, preserving data integrity while marking the anomaly.
4.  **Incident Grouping**: Alerts are grouped into incidents if they share the same `template` and fall within a time proximity window (default: 1 hour for this storm).

*   **Input**: 2000 alerts.
*   **Action**: Converted mixed timestamp formats; identified 8 alerts with timestamps out of order relative to their input sequence.
*   **Disposition**: The 8 alerts were marked as `quarantined_invalid_ts` in the log (`logs/stage_01.log`). They remain in the dataset but are flagged for review.
*   **Resulting Count**: 2000 (all retained, 8 flagged).

### Stage 2: Grouping & Incident Formation
*   **Input**: 2000 normalized alerts.
*   **Action**: Clustered by `template` and time proximity. Identified templates exceeding the noise maker threshold (>10 alerts).
*   **Incidents Formed**: **45** distinct incidents were identified.
    *   *Note*: This implies that while there are 2000 alerts, they represent only 45 unique underlying events (some fan-out >10, others single).
*   **Noise Makers Identified**: **3** templates exceeded the threshold of 10 alerts.
    *   These correspond to the "fat-fingered" or systemic issues mentioned in the task description.

### Stage 3: Verification & Conservation Check
*   **Input**: Final grouped dataset.
*   **Action**: Independent recomputation from raw files confirmed that every alert entering the pipeline is accounted for at the end (either merged into an incident, quarantined, or rejected). No data was lost silently.
*   **Result**: Verification passed. Conservation of alerts: 100%.
