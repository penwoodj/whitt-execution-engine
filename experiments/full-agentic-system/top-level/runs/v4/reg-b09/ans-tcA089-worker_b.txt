  Reason: Ensures more reliable crops are scheduled first in case of naming conflict; avoids scheduling inconsistent or low-reliability crops.

- Rule: Revocation rule removes a crop from succession schedule if its maturity_days range exceeds 120 days (long-term risk). Trigger: maturity_days_max > 120.  
  Reason: Prevents overcommitment to long-cycle crops that may not fit in the season; avoids scheduling impractical crops.

- Rule: Return rule reverts a crop’s planting window if it overlaps with another crop's transplant window and both are scheduled for same date. Trigger: sow_date or transplant_date conflict detected.  
  Reason: Avoids double-sowing or overlapping growth stages; maintains physical feasibility of garden layout.
