What surprised me:  
The trailing-slash difference in prod.PATH_VAR was not documented, yet it caused an outage once — this is a real-world edge case that should be flagged as high-risk. The rule for trailing slash differences was triggered only after normalization and value comparison, which means the system correctly caught it despite being unrecorded.
