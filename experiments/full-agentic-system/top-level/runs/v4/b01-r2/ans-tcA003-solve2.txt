### Phase 1: Rule Scan & State Triggers

1. **Rule: Checkpoint Banking**  
   *Trigger:* Job Start or Resume.  
   *Effect:* Banks a checkpoint at `start_minute + 2` and every subsequent even-running minute (absolute). A bank landing exactly on HIGH's arrival counts as valid.

2. **Rule: Preemption Accounting**  
   *Trigger:* High Priority job preempts Low Priority job.  
   *Effect:* Minutes run past the last checkpoint are marked `wasted`. The preempted job must restart from its last checkpoint boundary (or zero if no checkpoints). `owed = total_duration - banked_minutes`.

3. **Rule: Core Scheduling Tie-Break**  
   *Trigger:* Multiple Low jobs waiting for core.  
   *Effect:* A resumed LOW outranks a fresh LOW. The core never idles while a LOW waits.

4. **Rule: Job Completion & Clock Advance**  
   *Trigger:* Job finishes execution.  
   *Effect:* Next waiting job starts at the exact finish minute of the current job (`s+n`). Makespan is the maximum finish minute across all jobs (including HIGH). Order list includes only LOW jobs sorted by finish time.
