# Queue Lag Report: End-to-End Processing Exercise

## 1. Workspace Layout & Decisions

**Root:** `/tmp/opencode/queue-run`
*   `README.md`: Documents structure, paths, and decisions (idempotent run).
*   `rules.md`: Thresholds for lag calculation, burst windows, rebalance correlation logic.
*   `decisions.md`: Handling of missing snapshots, backward offsets, and throughput estimation assumptions.

**Scripts:** `/tmp/opencode/queue-run/scripts`
*   `01_lag_calc.sh`: Computes per-consumer/partition max, mean, burst lag.
*   `02_correlate_rebalance.sh`: Aligns lag spikes with 3 rebalance timestamps; estimates throughput (msg/min).
*   `03_verify.sh`: Independent recomputation from raw data to check field-by-field drift.

**Outputs:** `/tmp/opencode/queue-run/output`
*   `lag-report.md`: Final report with tables, paths, and methodology.
*   `lag.json`: Structured data for downstream consumption.
*   `decisions.log`: One-line log of mess types handled (missing snapshots, backward offsets
