Phase 1 RULE SCAN:

- Aging bump rule: triggers when a job waits ≥10 minutes; increases its priority by one level (low → medium, medium → high); triggered by arrival time and duration; applies to all jobs in queue after 10-minute wait.  
- Preemption rule: higher-priority job arrives or resumes before lower-priority job; the lower-priority job is preempted and moves to back of queue with its original state preserved; triggered when a new high-priority job enters the system.  
- Resume rule: if a job was preempted, it resumes execution immediately upon completion of the higher-priority job; triggered when a preemption event ends and the preempted job's duration is still valid.  
- Fairness check rule: monitors low-priority jobs to determine if any have waited beyond 30 minutes without running; if yes, records starvation; triggered at end of each minute in the walk.  
- No-aging rerun rule: runs identical simulation but disables aging; compares starved job count between with and without aging; triggered during stage 4 comparison.  
- Duration-zero job rule: when a job has duration zero, it is immediately marked as "done" and removed from queue; triggers at arrival; applies to all jobs with duration = 0.  
- Tie-breaker rule: if two jobs have identical arrival times (to microsecond), the one with lower ID wins; triggered during initial queue sorting; used for job ordering in tie cases.  
- Manual priority reassignment rule: a single job is manually flagged mid-walk to change priority from medium to high; triggers at timestamp 12:05:34; overrides all other rules for that instance.
