Phase 1 rule list:
- Rule: LOW1 (dur, start) — triggers at minute start; sets core to LOW1 if no higher priority job or waiting LOW with smaller original start; ties broken by job id order (LOW1 < LOW2).
- Rule: LOW2 (dur, start) — triggers at minute start; sets core to LOW2 if no higher priority job or waiting LOW with smaller original start; ties broken by job id order (LOW1 < LOW2).
- Rule: HIGH (arrive, dur) — triggers at arrive; holds core in [arrive, arrive+dur); overrides any LOW during this interval.
- Rule: Core transition on minute m — if no LOW waiting, core remains idle; otherwise, core goes to the waiting LOW with smallest original start (tie: resumed LOW beats fresh).
- Rule: LOW banks checkpoint at (core-get minute + 2) and every 2 RUNNING minutes after; absolute minutes; bank counts even if on HIGH arrival.
- Rule: Preemption — when a HIGH arrives, any running LOW is preempted; wasted += minutes run past last bank; owed = duration − banked.
- Rule: Job occupation — job starting at s with owed k occupies s to s+k−1 and finishes at s+k; next LOW starts at that finish minute.
- Rule: Makespan — largest finish minute (including HIGH's finish).
