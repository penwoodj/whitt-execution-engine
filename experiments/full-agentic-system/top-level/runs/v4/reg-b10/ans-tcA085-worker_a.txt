Phase 1 RULE SCAN:

- Aging bump rule: Triggers when a job waits for 10 minutes (600 seconds) in the queue; increases its priority by one level. Triggered by arrival time and duration of jobs.
- Preemption rule: A higher-priority job preempts a lower-priority job if it arrives before or at the same time as the lower-priority job, regardless of waiting time. Triggered when a new high-priority job enters the queue.
- Resume rule: When a preempted job is resumed, its state is restored to the point of interruption; no restart occurs. Triggered upon completion of a higher-priority job that was preempted.
- Refill rule: If a job has been waiting longer than 15 minutes (900 seconds), it is refilled with a new duration of 20% more than original, only if its priority is low. Triggered when wait time exceeds 900 seconds and priority is "low".
- Fairness check rule: Monitors whether any low-priority job has waited beyond 30 minutes (1800 seconds) without being scheduled; flags such jobs as starving. Triggered during each scheduling decision.
- Expiry rule: Jobs that have been in the queue for more than 20 minutes (1200 seconds) are removed from the system and marked expired. Triggered when a job's wait time exceeds 1200 seconds.
- Source-ranking rule: Assigns jobs to scheduler slots based on arrival order, with ties broken by priority level; if two jobs have identical arrival times, the one with higher priority is scheduled first. Triggered at every scheduling decision.
