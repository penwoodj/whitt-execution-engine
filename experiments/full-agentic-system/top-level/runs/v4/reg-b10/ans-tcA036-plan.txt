1. Parse SRT file strictly using regex and structured parsing rules; for each cue, extract start_time, end_time, index, text, and speaker label with explicit field validation; log malformed cues with their line number, raw content, and reason (e.g., missing timestamp, invalid format, malformed time string).  
2. For overlapping cues: compute exact overlap intervals between consecutive cues by comparing end_time of one to start_time of next; trim the later cue to match the earlier one’s end_time if overlap exists; extend durations under 0.5 seconds using a fixed rule (e.g., extend to 0.5s) and log original duration, new duration, and reason; renumber cues sequentially from 1 after all adjustments; preserve all text content exactly as read.  
3. For reading speed: compute words per second (WPS) for each cue by counting characters in text (excluding punctuation), divide by duration in seconds; if WPS > 21, split the cue at a fixed interval (e.g., every 50 characters or every 1/4 of total duration); extend cues with low WPS below 8 words per second to meet minimum readability threshold; log original WPS, new WPS, and split point.  
4. Generate defect report table: list all defects before and after each stage (overlaps, gaps >5s, durations <0.5s, numbering gaps, reading speed violations), with counts and exact cue indices; ensure the table is ordered by stage number and includes raw values for comparison; include a column indicating whether the defect was resolved or reported only.  
5. Output final files: repaired.srt (with all original lines preserved), repair-report.md (human-readable table of defects, before/after, with full cues flagged), defects.json (machine-readable, exact numbers, no rounding); ensure every number in output matches a raw value from the parsed data or intermediate step.

**Rules document:** rules.md (next to scripts) — contains all thresholds and decisions:  
- Overlap threshold: any end_time ≥ start_time of next cue  
- Duration under 0.5s: extend to exactly 0.5 seconds  
- WPS >21: split at every 50 characters or every 0.25 seconds (whichever comes first)  
- Gaps >5s: report only, do not fill  
- Numbering gaps: renumber sequentially after all fixes
