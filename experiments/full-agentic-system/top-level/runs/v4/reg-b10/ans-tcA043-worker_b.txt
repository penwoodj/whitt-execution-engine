- Rule: If a transaction amount is negative, it is flagged and categorized under "Negative Amount"; expires after one month of no such entries.  
  Trigger event: Amount < 0.

- Rule: If a merchant name matches a known pattern with high confidence (e.g., regex match), it is promoted to primary category; applies only when rule confidence > 95%.  
  Trigger event: Regex match with confidence score ≥ 95%.

- Rule: If no category is assigned after all rules, fallback to "Uncategorized"; expires after one month of no such entries.  
  Trigger event: All rules fail.

- Rule: Source-ranking rule: if a merchant appears in more than 3 transactions, it is ranked as primary source; applies only when frequency > 3.  
  Trigger event: Merchant appears in ≥4 transactions.

- Rule: Refill rule: if a transaction matches a known recurring pattern (e.g., same merchant + same amount), and occurs within 7 days of previous, it is marked as refill; applies to all such cases.  
  Trigger event: Same merchant and amount within 7 days of prior transaction.
