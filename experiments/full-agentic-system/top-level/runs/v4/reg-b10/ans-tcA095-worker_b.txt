Phase 1 RULE SCAN:

- Rule: Set wip-over-time daily sample from stage timestamps; triggered by any card entering a stage.  
  Event: Card enters backlog or doing or review or done (any stage entry).  

- Rule: Flag stuck cards over 14 days in a stage; triggered when a card remains in a stage beyond 14 days from its entry timestamp.  
  Event: Card remains in a stage with no exit timestamp after 14 days from entry.  

- Rule: Compute per-stage dwell time using start and end timestamps for each card; triggered by every card transition between stages (backlog→doing, doing→review, review→done).  
  Event: Card moves from one stage to the next in sequence.  

- Rule: Compute cycle-time distribution (median, p90, histogram) from entry to done timestamp; triggered when a card exits "done" stage.  
  Event: Card completes and exits "done" stage.  

- Rule: Handle loop-backs by multipass accounting — for each card that loops back from review to doing, increment doing dwell and cycle time with full path; triggered on any card transition from review → doing.  
  Event: Card moves from review to doing (loop-back).  

- Rule: Update flow stats per-stage time using timestamp pairs; triggered by every stage entry and exit event.  
  Event: Card enters or exits a stage.  

- Rule: Detect and flag invalid timestamps where done occurs before doing; triggered when a card has a "done" timestamp earlier than its "doing" timestamp.  
  Event: Card has a done timestamp < doing timestamp in any record.  

- Rule: Adjust stage names on rename mid-export — map old stage name to new one during processing; triggered by presence of stage name mismatch (e.g., "review" renamed to "approval").  
  Event: Stage name appears as "approval" instead of "review" in export data.  

- Rule: On expiry of a card's flow stats window, reset per-stage dwell and wip-over-time for that card; triggered when a card is marked expired (no activity after 30 days).  
  Event: Card has no stage entry or exit event for 30 consecutive days.  

- Rule: Fallback to default cycle time if timestamps are missing or invalid; triggered when any timestamp field is null or malformed.  
  Event: Timestamp field is empty, NaN, or out-of-order.  

- Rule: Source-ranking rule — assign priority to cards based on entry timestamp in backlog; triggered when card enters backlog.  
  Event: Card enters backlog stage.  

- Rule: Revocation of loop-back accounting if a card exits doing before review; triggered when a card leaves doing without entering review.  
  Event: Card exits doing stage with no subsequent entry into review.  

- Rule: Refill dwell time for cards that re-enter doing after being in review; triggered on any card returning to doing from review.  
  Event: Card returns to doing after being in review.  

- Rule: Return of stuck status if a card is aged beyond 14 days and has no exit path; triggered when a card remains in stage >14 days with no exit event.  
  Event: Card stays in stage longer than 14 days without transition out.
