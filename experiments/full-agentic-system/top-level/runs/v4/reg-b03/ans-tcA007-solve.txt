5.  **Incident Span Calculation Rule**: For each incident, the span is calculated as `max(timestamp) - min(timestamp)` after sorting. State Change: Incident Attribute $\to$ `SPAN`.
6.  **Service Blast Radius Rule**: The set of unique services involved in an incident is counted. State Change: Incident Attribute $\to$ `SERVICE_COUNT`.
