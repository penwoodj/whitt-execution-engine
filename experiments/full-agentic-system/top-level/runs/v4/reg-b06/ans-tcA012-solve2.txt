Phase 1 RULE SCAN:

- Rule: Missing check counts as downtime. Trigger: Any service with missing uptime check (no data) in any minute of a day.  
- Rule: Error budget burn rate per day is computed from actual errors vs. target SLA, using daily error minutes against monthly budget. Trigger: Daily availability report generation.  
- Rule: Budget exhaustion flag triggered when cumulative error minutes exceed monthly budget for a service. Trigger: End-of-day budget check after all checks are processed.  
- Rule: Burn rate over 3x sustainable burn is flagged as "high-risk" and recorded with day, service, and burn rate. Trigger: Daily burn rate calculation.  
- Rule: If any day has more than 10% of the monthly error budget consumed, flag it as "critical". Trigger: End-of-day budget consumption check.  
- Rule: When a service burns its entire monthly budget by day nine, record the exact time and reason (e.g., one bad hour vs. slow grind). Trigger: Day-nine budget exhaustion check.  
- Rule: Refill rule — if error budget is below 10% remaining at end of month, auto-refill to full monthly cap. Trigger: End-of-month budget review.  
- Rule: Fallback rule — if a service fails to meet SLA for three consecutive days, trigger fallback mechanism and log event with timestamp. Trigger: Three-day consecutive SLA failure.
