- Rule: Flag zombie subscriptions where charges occur after an apparent cancel gap (defined as absence of charge for ≥2× period). Triggered when a merchant shows activity post a gap equal to or exceeding twice its recurrence cycle.  
- Rule: Identify price hikes by detecting same merchant, same amount before and after a change point, with new amount higher than original. Triggered when transaction amount increases at the same merchant within a defined time window (≤30 days).

- Rule: Handle clock-skewed cycles explicitly — for example, a 28-day cycle that does not align with calendar months. Triggered when period is non-monthly (e.g., 28 days) and must be treated independently from monthly patterns.  
- Rule: Prevent merging of subscriptions with identical amount but different merchants. Triggered when two entries have same amount but differ in merchant name; both are retained as separate entities.
