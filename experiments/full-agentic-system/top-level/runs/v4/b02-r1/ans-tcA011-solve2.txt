### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
**Logic rules defined for retry storm detection and edge case handling.**

```text
RULE: GROUP_BY_REQUEST_ID - Group all records by `request_id` to calculate total attempts per logical request.
RULE: ATTEMPT_VALIDATION - Flag records with `attempt_number == 0` as "Invalid Start" (surfaces data quality issues).
RULE: STORM_ADJACENT_THRESHOLD - Identify requests where Total Attempts > 3 as "Storm-Adjacent".
RULE: STORM_CORE_THRESHOLD - Identify requests where Total Attempts > 10 as "Storm-Core".
RULE: TIMESTAMPS_EXTRACT - Extract earliest start, peak activity (highest count), and latest drain from storm windows.
RULE: OUTCOME_AGGREGATION - Count outcomes (timeout, 5xx, refused) specifically within the identified storm window.
```
