### Stage 2: Find Knee (Knee.md)
**Action:** Identified the point where latency growth rate exceeded throughput drop rate.
**Log:** `Stage 2 Complete: Knee identified at 600 users.`

*   **Method:** Calculated derivative of Latency vs. Users.
*   **Result:** At 590 users, P95 was ~400ms. At 610 users, P95 jumped to ~1,200ms while throughput only dropped slightly. This is the "knee."

### Stage 3: Graceful Degradation Claim (Verdict)
**Action:** Verified if system stabilized or scaled after knee point.
**Log:** `Stage 3 Complete: Verdict = FAILED.`

*   **Reality:** Throughput dropped from ~885 to ~450 req/s (approx 50% drop) with a massive latency spike (>1250ms). The system did not stabilize; it continued to worsen until total failure at 1000 users.
*   **Conclusion:** The claim is false.

## 6. One Honest Surprise

**Surprise:** The system failed completely at exactly 1000 users (Throughput = 0, Error % = 100%). This suggests a hard limit or resource exhaustion rather than a gradual degradation curve continuing beyond the knee point. The "graceful" part of the requirement was never reached; it was a binary switch from functional to dead at the maximum load.
