**Verdict:** **FAILED**.

2.  **Row B (>700):** Status `400 Bad Request` / Error Type `Connection Refused`. Expected: Rate limiting. Actual: Connection pool exhaustion/collapse.

### Stage 4: Error Taxonomy Over Ramp
**Dependency:** Requires Degradation Check (Stage 3).

-   **>600 Users:** `Timeout`, `Connection Refused`.
-   **Pattern:** Errors appear abruptly at the knee point, confirming lack of graceful degradation mechanisms (e.g., circuit breakers, backpressure) were active or effective.
