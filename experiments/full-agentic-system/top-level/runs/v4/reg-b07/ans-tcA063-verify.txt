## Rules.md

```markdown
# Rotation Policy Rules (Grandfather-Father-Son)

1. **Daily Backup**: Every day, a new daily snapshot is created with size ~100MB and 1% corruption chance per snapshot.
2. **Retention Tiers**:
   - Daily: Keep last 7 days (retained for 7 days).
   - Weekly: After 7 days, promote to weekly tier; keep 4 weeks (total 4 weeks retained).
   - Monthly: After 28 days, promote to monthly tier; keep 12 months.
3. **Promotion Ambiguity Rule**:
   - If a day is both weekly and monthly retention boundary (e.g., day 7 of month), the daily backup is promoted to weekly *first*, then to monthly only if it survives 28 days after that.
4. **Corruption Handling**:
   - When a retained snapshot corrupts, policy falls back to the next tier in sequence: daily → weekly → monthly.
   - If no valid tier exists (e.g., all daily backups corrupted), system logs error and marks day as "corruption cascade".
5. **Restore Drill Rules**:
   - Drills occur randomly every 10 days with probability 10% per drill.
   - A requested snapshot must exist *and* be intact to pass.
   - If a drill requests a snapshot that was never eligible (e.g., backup from day 3 of month, but no daily retention), it is logged as an edge case and fails.
6. **Corruption Rate**: Exactly 1% per snapshot, seeded with random generator `seed=42`.
7. **Ambiguity Handling**:
   - On overlapping boundaries (e.g., day 7 of a month), the daily backup is promoted to weekly on day 7, then to monthly only after 28 days.
8. **Edge Case Logging**:
   - All edge cases are logged with timestamp, rule name, and action taken.
9. **Verification Rule**: Every output must be re-computed from raw data; drift detected via `verify_all.py`.
10. **Idempotency**: All scripts are idempotent (re-run without side effects).
```
