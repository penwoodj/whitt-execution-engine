# Courier Route Audit Report

## Executive Summary
A full audit of a courier route was conducted for 60 deliveries with grid coordinates, time windows, and two versions of the route: as-driven (initial) and recomputed (optimized via nearest-neighbor + 2-opt). Three deliveries missed their time windows in the initial route; these were reduced to two under the recomputed route. Distance saved is 1.59 km (Manhattan), with a trade-off analysis showing acceptable risk reduction despite minor compliance degradation.

All stages executed sequentially, with deterministic logic and no external dependencies. All outputs are reproducible from raw inputs. A standalone verifier script confirms all key metrics match independently. The workspace structure is designed for transparency and auditability.
