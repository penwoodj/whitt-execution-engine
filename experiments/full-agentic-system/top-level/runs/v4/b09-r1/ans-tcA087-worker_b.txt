Phase 1 RULE SCAN:

- Rule: Book with zero pages is excluded from all active queues. Trigger: Book entry with page_count = 0 in input data.
- Rule: Mood tag overlap threshold of 75% triggers diversity downgrade; if two books share >75% mood tags, they are treated as same-mood and must be separated by at least one non-overlapping book. Trigger: Pair of books with overlapping mood tags exceeding 75% similarity.
- Rule: Series books must maintain internal reading order; any book in a series that is not read sequentially after the previous book in the series is rejected from the next-up plan. Trigger: Book has "series" tag and appears out-of-order relative to prior series book in the queue.
- Rule: Alternate fiction/nonfiction constraint enforces strict alternation starting with fiction on first slot; if a non-fiction book follows a fiction book, or vice versa, it is rejected unless explicitly allowed by metadata. Trigger: Book type (fiction/nonfiction) at any position in the 12-book plan.
- Rule: Max consecutive pages limit of 600 applies to all books in the queue; any book exceeding 600 pages is excluded from the next-up plan. Trigger: Book page_count > 600.
- Rule: Books with progress fraction > 50% are considered "started" and must be prioritized over unstarted books. Trigger: Progress_fraction > 0.5 in input data.
- Rule: After all started books are scheduled, the next-up plan is built from remaining eligible books using priority + topic diversity scoring. Trigger: All books with progress_fraction > 0.5 have been placed in the sequence.
- Rule: Topic diversity rule activates when a book shares more than 40% of its topics with any previously selected book; such books are rejected unless a gap of at least two unselected books is present between them. Trigger: A new book has topic overlap >40% with any already scheduled book in the plan.
- Rule: If a book is half-finished (progress_fraction = 0.5), it must be placed immediately after all other started books, and only one such book may exist in the final sequence. Trigger: progress_fraction == 0.5 for any book.
- Rule: Fallback rule: if no valid book can satisfy alternation or diversity constraints, return the most recently added book with lowest page count as a placeholder (only if all other rules fail). Trigger: All books fail to meet alternation, diversity, or page limits after full constraint check.
- Rule: Source-ranking rule: books from "public domain" source are ranked lower than commercial; if two books have equal score, public domain book is placed later. Trigger: Book has "source" tag with value "public domain".
- Rule: Revocation rule: any book that violates the series order constraint is removed from consideration and marked for revocation in output logs. Trigger: Series book appears out-of-order after a prior series book.
- Rule: Refill rule: if a book was previously excluded due to page limit or diversity, it may be reinserted into the queue only if all other books have been scheduled and no new constraints are violated. Trigger: All books in next-up plan are filled; refill condition met.
