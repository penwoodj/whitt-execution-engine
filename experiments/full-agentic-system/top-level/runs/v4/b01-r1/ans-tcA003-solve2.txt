### Phase 1: Rule Scan & State Triggers

1. **Rule:** `CheckpointBanking` (Absolute Minute = Start/Resume + 2).
   *Trigger:* Job starts or resumes execution.
   *Effect:* Establishes a checkpoint boundary at absolute minute $T_{bank}$.

2. **Rule:** `PeriodicCheckpointing` (Every 2 RUNNING Minutes).
   *Trigger:* Execution continues past the initial bank point.
   *Effect:* Updates checkpoint boundary to $T_{current\_minute} - T_{start/last\_resume} \equiv 0 \pmod 2$.

3. **Rule:** `PreemptionWasteCalculation`.
   *Trigger:* High priority job preempts Low priority job.
   *Effect:* Calculates wasted minutes = Current Minute - Last Banked Minute. Sets `owed` = Total Duration - Banked Minutes.

4. **Rule:** `CoreSchedulingPriority`.
   *Trigger:* End of minute, core becomes free or new job arrives.
   *Effect:* Assigns core to waiting Low job with smallest original start time (Resume > Fresh). If no Low jobs wait, assigns High job. Core never idles if a Low job is waiting.

5. **Rule:** `JobCompletion`.
   *Trigger:* Job finishes execution (`owed` minutes elapsed).
   *Effect:* Updates makespan to current minute + 1. Frees core for next waiting job immediately in the same finish min.
