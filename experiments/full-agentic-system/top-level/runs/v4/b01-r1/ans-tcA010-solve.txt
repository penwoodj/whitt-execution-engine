### **Rules.md**
```markdown
# Rules for Queue-Lag Analysis

1. **Lag Calculation**:  
   - Lag = Log End Offset - Consumer Offset.  
   - Max/mean lag per consumer/partition over time, including burst windows (lag > 2x mean).  

2. **Rebalance Correlation**:  
   - Correlate lag spikes with rebalance timestamps within ±5 minutes.  
   - A spike is valid if it occurs immediately after a rebalance and exceeds the pre-rebalance mean by >30%.  

3. **Throughput Estimation**:  
   - Throughput = (Offset Delta) / Time Interval.  
   - Messages per minute = Offset Delta / 60 seconds.  

4. **Anomaly Detection**:  
   - Missing snapshots: Flag as "MISSING_SNAPSHOT" and interpolate using linear regression of neighboring points.  
   - Backward offsets: Flag as "BACKWARD_OFFSET" and treat as a reset event.  

5. **Burst Windows**:  
   - Burst window = Continuous period where lag > 2x mean for ≥3 consecutive snapshots.  

6. **Worker-2 Verdict**:  
   - Worker-2 is "slow" if its mean lag exceeds the global mean by >50%.  
   - Worker-2 is "stuck" if its lag spikes correlate with hot partitions (partition-specific lag > 80% of max).  

7. **Precision Rules**:  
   - No rounding: All numbers must be exact (e.g., 12.3456, not 12.35).  
   - Percentages: Always include denominators (e.g., "12% of 1000 messages").  

8. **Error Handling**:  
   - Errors must specify what failed and where (e.g., "MISSING_SNAPSHOT at t=45m, partition=2").  
   - Exit nonzero on any error or verification failure.
```
