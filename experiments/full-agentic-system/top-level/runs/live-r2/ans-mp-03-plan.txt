Based on current research trajectories, here are the steps for a deep dive into "Agentic Systems with Models at Much Smaller Scale":

1.  **Define the Baseline:** Establish what constitutes "much smaller scale" (e.g., <7B parameters) and contrast it with standard agentic baselines (e.g., Llama-3-70B, GPT-4).
2.  **Analyze Efficiency Mechanisms:** Investigate how small models achieve high performance through techniques like MoE (Mixture of Experts), quantization, or specialized instruction tuning that reduces the need for massive context windows.
3.  **Evaluate Agentic Capabilities:** Assess specific agent behaviors in small models: tool use reliability, multi-step reasoning chains, and self-correction loops compared to larger counterparts.
4.  **Review Empirical Proofs:** Examine published benchmarks (e.g., AgentBench, GSM8K) where smaller models have demonstrated surprising parity or outperformance due to architectural efficiency rather than raw parameter count.
5.  **Identify Progress Frontiers:** Pinpoint areas of active research: hybrid architectures (small backbone + large tool interface), dynamic scaling strategies, and domain-specific fine-tuning that maximizes small model utility.
