### Phase 1: RULE SCAN
The following rules govern state changes during the night's events (arrivals, grants, preemptions, expiries):

1.  **Arrival Rule**: `HIGH` arrives at minute 5; triggers immediate preemption of any running `LOW`.
2.  **Grant/Start Rule**: `LOW` jobs start or resume in order of their absolute start minutes (`s`). A resumed job outranks a fresh one at ties.
3.  **Checkpoint Bank Rule**: Checkpoints bank at `start_or_resume + 2` and every subsequent even running minute (absolute time). Banks landing exactly on the preemption trigger count; work past the last bank is wasted.
4.  **Preemption Expiry Rule**: Upon preemption, minutes run since the last valid bank are marked as "wasted" and must be redone upon resume. The grant effectively expires for those specific minutes before the next start.
5.  **Completion Order Rule**: `LOW` jobs are listed by their absolute finish minute (`s + duration`).
