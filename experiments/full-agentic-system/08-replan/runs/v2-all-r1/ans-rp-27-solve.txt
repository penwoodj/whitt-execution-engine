### Phase 1: Rule Scan
The following rules govern the state changes during the event list (arrivals, grants, preemptions, expiries):

1.  **Arrival/Grant Rule**: LOW jobs are granted the core starting at their specified minute `s` for duration `n`, occupying minutes `[s, s+n-1]`.
2.  **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arriving at minute `t` instantly preempts any running LOW job; no other job runs while HIGH is active.
3.  **Checkpoint/Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` and every subsequent even number of *running* minutes (absolute time). Banks landing exactly on HIGH's arrival count as valid.
4.  **Waste/Resume Rule**: On preemption, work past the last banked minute is wasted. The resumed job owes `total_duration - banked_minutes`.
5.  **Scheduling Priority Rule**: When multiple LOW jobs wait, the one with the earlier start minute runs first; if start minutes tie, the resumed job outranks a fresh one.
6.  **Completion Order Rule**: LOW jobs are listed by their finish minute (absolute time). HIGH is excluded from this list.
