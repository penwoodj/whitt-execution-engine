### Phase 1: RULE SCAN
The following rules govern state changes during the night's events (arrivals, grants, preemptions, expiries):

1.  **Arrival Rule**: `HIGH` arrives at minute 3; triggers immediate preemption of any running `LOW` job and halts all other execution until `HIGH` completes.
2.  **Grant/Start Rule**: `LOW` jobs start or resume in order of their absolute start minutes (`s`). A resumed `LOW` outranks a fresh one at ties. If the core is busy, waiting `LOW`s queue up.
3.  **Checkpoint Bank Rule**: Checkpoints bank at `start_or_resume + 2` and every subsequent even running minute (absolute time). Banks landing exactly on `HIGH`'s arrival count; no work is wasted if a bank aligns with the preemption trigger.
4.  **Preemption Waste Rule**: On preemption, minutes run past the last valid bank are wasted (`owed = total_duration - banked_minutes`). Work must be redone after resume.
5.  **Core Continuity Rule**: The core never idles while a `LOW` job is waiting; the next waiting `LOW` starts in the exact minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
