### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
This phase lists every rule that changes state during the event processing, identifying the specific claim (event) that triggers it.

1.  **Source Hierarchy Rule**: When a higher source contradicts a lower one, the higher source wins and the lower source's assertion counts FALSE; triggered by **c2** (Signed Handoff vs Machine Log).
2.  **Hearsay/Single Source Verification Rule**: A claim resting only on hearsay or only on the KNOWN-BUGGY dashboard is UNVERIFIED; triggered by **c4** (Hallway Hearsay) and **c5** (Buggy Dashboard).
3.  **Agreement Truth Rule**: When sources agree, the claim is TRUE; triggered by **c1** (Machine Log alone implies agreement with itself/ground truth) and **c3** (Signed Handoff + Hallway Hearsay).
