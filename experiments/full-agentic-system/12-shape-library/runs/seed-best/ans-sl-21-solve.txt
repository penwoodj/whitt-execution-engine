**Phase 1 RULE SCAN**
- **Load Execution Rule**: A load command is executed if there is sufficient free space (>= requested size). If not, it triggers eviction/deferral logic.
- **Eviction Selection Rule**: Evictions target the Least Recently Used (LRU) unpinned model that fits in the available space to make room for the new load.
- **Pinned Model Protection Rule**: Pinned models (Model B) are never evicted, regardless of LRU status or space constraints.
- **Deferred Load Rule**: If a load cannot fit even after evicting all unpinned models (and pinned models remain), the load is deferred. The `deferred` list contains the ID of this specific load command.
- **Count Definitions**: `loads` counts successfully executed loads; `evictions` counts successful eviction operations performed to make space; `deferrals` counts loads that were deferred due to lack of space after max evictions.
