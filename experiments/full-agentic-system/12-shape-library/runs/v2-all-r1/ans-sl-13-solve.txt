### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
This phase lists every rule that changes state during the event sequence (Arrivals), including expiry, return, revocation, refill, promotion, fallback, or source-ranking rules, marked with their trigger events.

1.  **Per-Attempt Cost Rule**: Triggers on `Arrival` of an attempt; deducts cost from budget immediately upon arrival.
2.  **Wait Time Cost Rule**: Triggers on `Arrival` of a wait interval (between attempts); deducts duration in seconds from budget immediately upon arrival.
3.  **Budget Suppression Rule (Primary)**: Triggers if `Current Spend > Budget`; suppresses job and doubles budget for re-check.
4.  **Budget Re-Check Rule**: Triggers after doubling; determines final suppression status based on doubled budget vs spend.
5.  **Expiry Return Rule**: Triggers upon completion of the trigger request (successful attempt); returns half of the *granted* amount, rounded down, immediately before processing subsequent events.
