### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
This phase lists every rule in the task that changes state during the event sequence (tonight's events), including expiry, return, revocation, refill, promotion, fallback, or source-ranking rules, marked with their trigger.

1.  **Retry Wait Rule**: Triggers on each failed attempt; sets wait time to $3 \times 2^{(n-1)}$ seconds for the $n$-th failure (observed sequence: 3s, 6s, 12s).
2.  **Attempt Cost Rule**: Triggers on every retry execution; deducts 3 tokens from budget per attempt.
3.  **Wait Cost Rule**: Triggers after each failed attempt's wait period completes; deducts 1 token from budget per second of wait time.
4.  **Budget Suppression Rule (Primary)**: Triggers if total spend exceeds initial budget (30); suppresses the job immediately.
5.  **Budget Refill Rule**: Triggers exactly once after a suppression event; doubles the current budget limit and re-evaluates the suppression condition against the new limit.
6.  **Success Condition Rule**: Triggers on the 4th attempt; ends the sequence successfully if budget allows.
