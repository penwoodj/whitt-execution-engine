### Phase 1: RULE SCAN
Based on the task description and "tonight's" rules (ignoring orientation story numbers), here are the state-changing rules triggered by the events `load A`, `load C`, `load D`, `unload D`:

1.  **Load Rule**: Triggered by `load X`. If capacity allows, model `X` is loaded into resident set; if not, evictions occur (see Eviction Rule).
2.  **Eviction Rule**: Triggered by a failed Load. Evicts least-recently-touched **unpinned** models one-by-one until space fits or only pinned models remain.
3.  **Deferral Rule**: Triggered by a failed Load where only pinned models remain and no space exists. Model `X` is deferred (no eviction, no load).
4.  **Touch/Recency Rule**: Triggered by any successful Load or Unload of model `X`. Moves `X` to most-recent position in the resident set.
5.  **Unload Rule**: Triggered by `unload X`. Frees capacity equal to size of `X` if `X` is resident; otherwise, it is a no-op.
