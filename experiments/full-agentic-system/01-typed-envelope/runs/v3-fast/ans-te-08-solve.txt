### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Rules that change state during the event or claim list:*

1. **Stage Completion Rule**: A clean observation at position $P$ completes stage $P$, setting `rollout_pct` to $10 \times P$. Triggered by: Clean observations (Pos 1, Pos 3).
2. **Breach Penalty Rule**: A breaching observation spends the step and does not advance stages; state reverts to last completed stage values. Triggered by: Breach at Pos 2, Breach at Pos 4.
3. **Limit Tightening Rule**: After any breach, the limit for all *remaining* steps tightens to 0.8%. Triggered by: Breach at Pos 2 (affects Pos 3), Breach at Pos 4 (affects End).
4. **Stop Condition Rule**: A second breach triggers status `STOPPED`, holding state at last completed stage. Triggered by: Breach at Pos 4 (given previous breach at Pos 2).
5. **Pause Condition Rule**: Exactly one breach and list ends triggers status `PAUSED`. Triggered by: N/A (Condition not met due to second breach).
6. **Done Condition Rule**: No breaches and list ends triggers status `DONE`. Triggered by: N/A.
