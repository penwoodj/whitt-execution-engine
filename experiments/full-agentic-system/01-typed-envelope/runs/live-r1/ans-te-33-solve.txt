### Phase 1: RULE SCAN

**Rule List & Triggers:**
1. **Arrival Rule**: Jobs arrive in strict order (HIGH @ t=0, LOW1 @ t=0, LOW2 @ t=2).
2. **Preemption Rule**: HIGH preempts instantly upon arrival; LOW jobs are suspended immediately if a job is running when HIGH arrives or if they are currently running.
3. **Completion Order Rule**: The `order` list must strictly follow the finish time of the LOW jobs (earliest finish first).
4. **Waste Calculation Rule**: For any preempted LOW job, waste = (Current Run Time) - (Last Checkpoint Time). Work done between checkpoints is lost.
5. **Checkpoint Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes. State resets to the last checkpoint time upon resumption.
6. **Resumption Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW job outranks a fresh one at any tie (relevant for scheduling logic, though here only one HIGH exists).
7. **Makespan Definition Rule**: Makespan = Finish Time of HIGH - Start Time of First Job (LOW1).
