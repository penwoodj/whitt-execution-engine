### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
This phase lists every rule in the task that changes state during the event or claim list, along with the specific trigger.

1. **Rule: Step Limit Check** (Triggered by: Every Observation Arrival)
   - Logic: Compare observed error rate against the current step's limit. Limits are 2.0% for steps 1-5 and 1.0% for steps 6-10. If breached, trigger rollback logic; otherwise, mark stage complete and update rollout percentage.

2. **Rule: Rollback Logic** (Triggered by: Breach)
   - Action: Pause climb, revert to previous step number. The current observation is marked "spent".
   - Side Effect: Limit for *every remaining step* tightens to 0.8%.

3. **Rule: Stage Progression & Rollout Calculation** (Triggered by: Clean Observation)
   - Action: Increment stage counter ($S$). Update rollout percentage = $10 \times S$.
   - Note on Post-Rollback Resumption: If resuming, the next clean observation completes the *next* stage number after the one held during rollback.

4. **Rule: Double-Breach Termination** (Triggered by: Second Breach)
   - Action: Status becomes "STOPPED". Hold current step number. Stop all further processing.

5. **Rule: Completion Logic** (Triggered by: Successful completion of Step 10)
   - Action: If no breaches occur and steps are exhausted, Status becomes "DONE".
