### Phase 1 RULE SCAN

1.  **Climb Step Rule**: The rollout climbs in steps; each step has an associated error rate limit based on its position (Steps 1–5: ≤2.0%, Steps 6–10: ≤1.0%).
2.  **Breach Pause Rule**: If an observed error rate exceeds the current step's limit, the climb pauses immediately.
3.  **Rollback Rule**: Upon a breach, the system rolls back to the previous step (the last successfully verified step).
4.  **Observation Spent Rule**: The specific observation that caused the breach is marked "spent" and does not trigger re-evaluation against the new tighter limit; it is discarded.
5.  **Tighten Limit Rule**: After any rollback, the error rate limit for *every remaining step* tightens to 0.8%.
6.  **Resume Observation Rule**: The climb resumes starting from the observation immediately following the spent/breached one.
7.  **Second Breach Stop Rule**: If a second breach occurs (any subsequent breach after the first), the rollout status becomes "STOPPED", holding the current step, and no further steps are attempted.
8.  **Completion Rule**: If zero breaches occur during the entire climb sequence, the status is "DONE".
