### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Listing every rule that changes state during the event stream, triggered by specific events.*

1.  **Load Rule**: Triggered by `load A`. Action: Allocates capacity (3GB) from budget; if space insufficient, evicts LRU unpinned models until fit or deferral occurs.
2.  **Pinned Rule**: Triggered by `load C` and implicitly present for all states. Action: Model C is pinned and never evicted regardless of pressure or age.
3.  **Load Rule**: Triggered by `load D`. Action: Allocates capacity (2GB) from remaining budget; checks fit against current resident set.
4.  **Unload Rule**: Triggered by `unload D`. Action: Frees capacity (2GB), removes Model D from active list, returns to "available" state immediately (no expiry/return logic applies here as this is an explicit unload command, not a budget limit trigger).
