### Phase 1: RULE SCAN

1. **Arrival Rule**: Jobs arrive in strict order; state updates immediately upon arrival of each job/event, no overlapping processing or pre-computation allowed.
2. **Preemption Rule**: The HIGH priority job preempt instantly at its start time (minute 6), interrupting any running LOW job regardless of its current progress or checkpoint status.
3. **Checkpoint Rule**: Each LOW job checkpoints every 2 completed minutes; work done beyond the last valid checkpoint is considered wasted if preemption occurs before completion.
4. **Resumption Rule**: A resumed LOW job (one that was preempted and has a valid prior checkpoint) outranks a fresh LOW job (one starting anew) in any scheduling tie or conflict.
5. **Makespan Calculation Rule**: Makespan counts from the first start time (minute 0 by LOW1) to the last finish time of all jobs, including both HIGH and LOW jobs.
6. **Wasted Time Rule**: Wasted minutes are calculated as the difference between the total runtime of a preempted LOW job and its most recent checkpoint value before preemption occurred.
7. **Completion Order Rule**: The completion order lists only the LOW jobs (not the HIGH job) in the sequence they finished execution, prioritizing resumed jobs over fresh ones if ties occur.
