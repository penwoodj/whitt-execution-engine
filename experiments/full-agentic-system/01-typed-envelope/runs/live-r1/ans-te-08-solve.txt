### Phase 1 RULE SCAN

The following rules govern state changes during the event sequence. Rules are listed in order of priority (higher list = applied first) as per "pick the one higher in the list."

1.  **Breach Rule**: If `observed_error_rate` > `current_limit`, trigger **PAUSE/ROLLBACK**.

    *   *Effect*: For every remaining step in the climb, `current_limit` becomes **0.8**.

4.  **Completion Rule**: If events process without triggering any Breach Rule, Status becomes **DONE** after the final step.
