### Phase 1: RULE SCAN
*Scanning all rules that change state during the event sequence.*

1. **Arrival Rule**: Events are processed in strict arrival order; no two events share a line or trigger simultaneously.
2. **HIGH Job Execution Rule**: HIGH job starts immediately, runs to completion (6 minutes), and preempts any other running job instantly upon start.
3. **LOW Job Checkpoint Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of actual runtime; work past the last checkpoint is wasted if preempted.
4. **Preemption Loss Rule**: Upon HIGH job start, currently running LOW jobs are cut off immediately; their remaining time counts as "wasted".
5. **Resumption Priority Rule**: A resumed (preempted then restarted) LOW job outranks a fresh LOW job starting at the same moment or later.
6. **Makespan Definition Rule**: Makespan is calculated from the first start (minute 0) to the absolute last finish of any job.
7. **Completion Order Rule**: The output list reflects the order in which LOW jobs finish, respecting resumption priority for ties or overlaps.
