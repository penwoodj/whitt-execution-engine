### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
The following rules govern state changes during tonight's events. Each line lists the rule, its trigger condition (Event/Claim), and the resulting state change.

1. **Rule: Job Arrival & Scheduling**
   - Trigger: HIGH job arrival at minute 0.
   - Change: Schedules HIGH for execution; LOW1 enters "fresh" pending state immediately.
2. **Rule: Preemption Priority (High > Low)**
   - Trigger: HIGH starts execution and LOW jobs are ready/resumed.
   - Change: Interrupts any running or ready LOW job to execute HIGH immediately.
3. **Rule: Instant Preemption Completion**
   - Trigger: HIGH begins at minute 0 with duration 6 mins.
   - Change: HIGH runs to completion (minutes 0-5) without interruption because no other job is scheduled to start before it finishes? *Correction based on timeline*: LOW1 starts at min 0, LOW2 at min 3. HIGH preempts instantly. So LOW1/LOW2 are paused or skipped depending on exact overlap logic. Given "preempts instantly and runs to completion", HIGH occupies the core from t=0 to t=6. Any LOW jobs overlapping this window cannot run their full duration in that first block.
4. **Rule: LOW Job Checkpointing**
   - Trigger: A LOW job accumulates 2 completed minutes of work.
   - Change: Saves state; resets progress counter for the current batch (waste calculation basis).
5. **Rule: Waste Calculation on Preemption**
   - Trigger: HIGH preempts a LOW job before it reaches its last checkpoint or completion.
   - Change: Calculates `work_done_since_last_checkpoint` as wasted minutes. Resets LOW job state to last checkpoint.
6. **Rule: Job Resumption Priority (Resumed > Fresh)**
   - Trigger: Tie situation where both a resumed LOW and a fresh LOW are eligible for the core.
   - Change: The resumed LOW job executes before the fresh LOW job.
7. **Rule: Makespan Definition**
   - Trigger: End of all job executions.
   - Change: Calculates time from first start (min 0) to last finish.
