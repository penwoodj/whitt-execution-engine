### Phase 1 RULE SCAN

Here are the rules that change state during tonight's events, listed in order of priority (as per "pick the one higher in the list"), with their triggers:

1.  **Rule: Arrival & Stage Assignment** – Each arriving observation carries the next sequential stage number ($S$) and begins testing against the current limit for that step. Triggered by every event arrival.
2.  **Rule: Clean Observation Completion** – If error $\le$ Limit, the stage is completed. The rollout advances to the next step with a new Stage Number ($S+1$). Limit resets based on the new step range (Steps 1-5: 2.0%, Steps 6+: 1.0%). Triggered by an observation where Error $\le$ Limit.
3.  **Rule: Breach & Rollback** – If error > Limit, the climb pauses/rolls back to the previous step ($S_{prev}$). The current observation is spent. Status becomes PAUSED if this is the first breach; remains PAUSED for subsequent breaches. Triggered by an observation where Error > Limit (First or Second).
4.  **Rule: Limit Tightening** – After *any* rollback, the limit for *every remaining step* tightens to 0.8%. Triggered immediately after a Breach event is processed.
5.  **Rule: Observation Spent / Next Observation Triggers** – A breached observation does not re-test against the new tighter limit; only the NEXT arriving observation triggers a test against the current active stage and limit. Triggered by the arrival of the next event after a breach.
6.  **Rule: Second Breach Termination** – If a second breach occurs, status becomes STOPPED. The rollout halts immediately holding the previous step number. Triggered by the second observation where Error > Limit.
