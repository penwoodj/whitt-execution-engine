### Phase 1: RULE SCAN

The following rules govern the state changes during tonight's shift. Each line lists the rule logic and the specific event or claim that triggers it.

1. **Arrival Rule**: Upon an arrival (HIGH, LOW1, LOW2), the system records the job start time and initial progress; triggered by the *arrival event* of each job in strict order.
2. **Preemption Trigger Rule**: When the HIGH job starts at minute 0, it instantly preempts any running or queued jobs; triggered by the *start of the HIGH job*.
3. **HIGH Execution Rule**: The HIGH job runs to completion uninterrupted for its full duration (8 minutes); triggered by the *arrival and start* of the HIGH job.
4. **LOW Checkpoint Logic**: LOW jobs track progress in 2-minute increments; a checkpoint is logically recorded at t=0, t=2, t=4, etc.; triggered by the *passage of time* within a LOW job's execution window.
5. **Wasted Work Rule**: If a LOW job is preempted, any work done past the last valid checkpoint (multiple of 2) is marked as wasted; triggered by the *preemption event*.
6. **Resumption Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW job (one that was interrupted and will restart) outranks a fresh LOW job (new arrival) in scheduling order at any time tie; triggered by the *comparison of pending jobs* upon HIGH completion or new arrivals.
7. **LOW Execution Completion Rule**: A resumed LOW job continues from its last checkpoint, not zero; triggered by the *re-scheduling of the preempted job*.
8. **Fresh Start Logic**: A fresh LOW job starts at full speed from minute 0 relative to its own arrival window; triggered by the *arrival event* of a new LOW job while others are waiting or completed.
9. **Makespan Calculation Rule**: The makespan is calculated as the time difference between the first start (minute 0) and the last finish among all jobs; triggered by the *completion of the final job*.
10. **Order Determination Rule**: The completion order list records jobs strictly in the sequence they reach a "finished" state, ignoring idle gaps or preemption history; triggered by the *finish event* of each job.
