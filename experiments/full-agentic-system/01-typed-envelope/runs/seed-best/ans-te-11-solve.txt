### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Listing every rule that changes state during the event sequence, including limits, stages, status, and expiry logic.*

1.  **Step Limit Rule**: Steps 1–5 allow up to 2.0% error; Steps 6–10 allow up to 1.0% error. (Triggered by arrival of observation at current step).
2.  **Breach Definition**: If observed error rate > Step Limit, the event is a breach. (Triggered by comparison in Rule 1).
3.  **Rollback Action**: Upon breach, rollout pauses and rolls back to the previous step number. (Triggered by breach confirmation).
4.  **Observation Expiry**: The observation that caused the breach becomes "spent" and does not re-test against limits. (Triggered immediately upon breach).
5.  **Tightened Limit Rule**: After any rollback, the limit for *every remaining step* tightens to 0.8%. (Triggered by completion of a rollback event).
6.  **Stage Completion Rule**: A clean observation completes its current stage; rollout then advances to show 10% times that stage number. (Triggered by clean observation at end of stage).
7.  **Resume Logic**: After a rollback, the climb resumes from the next observation (the one after the spent breach). (Triggered by arrival of next valid observation).
8.  **Second Breach Rule**: A second breach stops everything; status becomes STOPPED; rollout holds current step. (Triggered by second breach confirmation).
9.  **Completion Rule**: If no breach occurs throughout the list, status is DONE and climb finishes at final calculated percentage. (Triggered by end of event list with clean state).
