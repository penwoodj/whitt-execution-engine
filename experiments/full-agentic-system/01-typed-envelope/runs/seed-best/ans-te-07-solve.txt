### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
1. **Stage Completion Rule**: A clean observation at position $N$ completes Stage $N$.
2. **Rollout Calculation Rule**: Upon completing a stage, rollout becomes $10 \times (\text{Completed Stage Number})$.
3. **Limit Assignment Rule (Steps 1-5)**: Limit is 2.0% for observations at positions 1 through 5.
4. **Limit Assignment Rule (Steps 6-10)**: Limit is 1.0% for observations at positions 6 through 10.
5. **Breach Handling Rule**: If Rate > Limit, the observation is spent (no stage completion), and the system holds the last completed state. The limit tightens to 0.8% for all *remaining* steps.
6. **Termination Rule (Second Breach)**: A second breach immediately sets status to STOPPED, holding the current state at the moment of failure.
7. **Termination Rule (End of List with One Breach)**: If the list ends after exactly one breach, status is PAUSED, holding the last completed stage's values.
8. **Completion Rule (No Breaches)**: If no breaches occur and the list ends, status is DONE.
