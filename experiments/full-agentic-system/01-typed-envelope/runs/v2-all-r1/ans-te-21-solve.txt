*Listing every rule that changes state during the event sequence, including triggers.*

1. **Rule: Attempt Execution** – Triggered by each failed attempt; increments `attempts` count and deducts cost (2 tokens) from `budget`.
2. **Rule: Wait Interval Enforcement** – Triggered after a failed attempt before the next retry; adds duration to `wait_s` total based on current wait value (starts at 2s, doubles each time).
3. **Rule: Budget Depletion Check** – Triggered after every cost deduction; if remaining budget < required for next step, triggers suppression logic.
4. **Rule: Suppression Logic** – Triggered when spend exceeds budget; job is suppressed immediately upon exceeding limit (no doubling of budget occurs mid-stream unless specified as a separate event, which it is not here).
5. **Rule: Success Condition** – Implicitly triggered on the 4th attempt (after 3 failures); if reached without suppression, `suppressed` remains false.
