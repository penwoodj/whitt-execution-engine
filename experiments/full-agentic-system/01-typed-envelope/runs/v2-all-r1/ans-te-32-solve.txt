### Phase 1: Rule Scan
The following rules govern the state changes during the event sequence (LOW1 start, LOW2 start, HIGH arrival/preemption, LOW1 resume/finish, LOW2 finish):

1.  **Arrival Order Rule**: Jobs are queued in strict order of their requested start minutes; a resumed job outranks a fresh job at any tie.
2.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: A LOW job banks (safes) its progress every 2 completed running minutes relative to its absolute clock time (start or resume).
3.  **Preemption Waste Rule**: Upon HIGH arrival, if a LOW is running, the work past the last checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume.
4.  **Bank Safety Rule**: If a checkpoint lands exactly on the minute HIGH arrives, that banked progress is safe (0 minutes wasted).
5.  **Resume Logic Rule**: A resumed LOW continues from its last bank; owed work = total duration - banked duration.
6.  **Core Utilization Rule**: The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts immediately when the current one finishes or is preempted.
