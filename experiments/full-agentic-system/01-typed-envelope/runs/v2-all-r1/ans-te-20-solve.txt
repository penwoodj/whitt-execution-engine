### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
This phase lists every rule in the task that changes state during the event sequence (tonight's events), excluding the "orientation" story numbers.

1.  **Retry Wait Rule**: After a failed attempt, wait time doubles ($3s \to 6s \to 12s$). Triggered by: Failed Attempt.
2.  **Attempt Cost Rule**: Each retry attempt costs 3 tokens. Triggered by: Retry Attempt Execution.
3.  **Wait Cost Rule**: Each second of waiting costs 1 token. Triggered by: Wait Period Elapse.
4.  **Budget Check Rule**: Total spend (attempts + waits) is compared against the budget (30). If exceeded, job is suppressed. Triggered by: End of Attempt Sequence / Budget Calculation.
5.  **Suppression Rule**: If spend > budget, suppress job and double budget exactly once for re-check. Triggered by: Budget Exceeded Event.
6.  **Success Condition Rule**: Job succeeds on the 4th attempt (after 3 failures). Triggered by: Successful Attempt Execution.
