Phase 1 RULE SCAN:
1. Arrivals in order: Jobs queue upon arrival if core busy; HIGH preempts current LOW immediately.
2. Execution exclusivity: Only one job runs on the core at any time (HIGH blocks all LOWs).
3. Checkpointing rule: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 minutes of *running* time; work past last checkpoint is wasted on preemption/resume.
4. Resumption precedence: A resumed LOW outranks a fresh LOW starting at the same minute.
5. Start order definition: LOW jobs take core priority based on their start minute (earlier start = higher rank).
6. Completion tracking: Track finish times and completion order of LOW jobs only.
7. Wasted time accumulation: Sum minutes lost due to preemption past the last checkpoint for each resumed job.
8. Makespan definition: Time from first start (minute 0) to last finish of any job.
