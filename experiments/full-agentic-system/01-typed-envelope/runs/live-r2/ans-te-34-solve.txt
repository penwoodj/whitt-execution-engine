### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Identifying rules that change state during the event sequence.*

1. **Job Creation Rule**: A LOW job enters the `pending` queue upon arrival at its start minute, ordered by `(start_minute, resume_status)`. Trigger: Arrival of LOW1 (t=0), then LOW2 (t=3).
2. **Preemption Rule**: Upon arrival of a HIGH job, if the core is busy or pending with jobs, all current/pending work halts immediately. Trigger: Arrival of HIGH (t=3).
3. **Waste Calculation Rule**: If preemption occurs after a checkpoint, `wasted_minutes` increases by `(current_run_time - last_checkpoint_time)`. Trigger: Preemption at t=3 (LOW1 has run 0 mins, so waste = 0; LOW2 hasn't started). *Correction*: LOW1 runs from 0 to 4. HIGH arrives at 3. At t=3, LOW1 has run 3 minutes. Last checkpoint was at 2 minutes. Waste = 3 - 2 = 1 min.
4. **Resume Rule**: Resumed jobs restart execution from the last completed checkpoint time. Trigger: Resume of LOW1 (t=6), Resume of LOW2 (after HIGH finishes).
5. **Completion Rule**: A job transitions to `completed` when its accumulated run time equals its duration, updating global state counters (`makespan`, `completion_order`). Trigger: Finish of LOW1 (t=8), Finish of LOW2 (t=9), Finish of HIGH (t=9).
