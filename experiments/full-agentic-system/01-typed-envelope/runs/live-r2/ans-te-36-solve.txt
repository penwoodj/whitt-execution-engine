### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*List of rules that change state during tonight's events:*

1. **Arrival Rule**: When a job arrives, it enters a queue if the core is busy or starts immediately if free; HIGH arrivals preempt any running LOW job instantly.
2. **Execution Rule**: LOW jobs consume 4 minutes of runtime; HIGH jobs consume their specified duration (3 min) and block all other execution while running.
3. **Checkpoint Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of actual running time on the core.
4. **Preemption Loss Rule**: Upon preemption by a HIGH job, any work done after the last checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume; the wasted amount is `current_running_time - last_checkpoint_time`.
5. **Completion Order Tracking**: The system tracks the finish order of LOW jobs (resumed or fresh) relative to each other.

**Triggers:**
- Event 0: Arrival LOW1 (Starts immediately).
- Event 3: Arrival LOW2 (Queues behind LOW1).
- Event 5: Arrival HIGH (Preempts running LOW job; triggers loss calculation if past checkpoint).
- Event 9: Completion of HIGH (Core becomes free for queued/resumed LOW jobs).
