Phase 1 RULE SCAN
- Arrival Rule: Jobs arrive at specified start minutes (LOW1 at 0, LOW2 at 0, HIGH at 2) and add entries to the job queue keyed by their start minute; ties resolved by insertion order of arrival.
- Preemption Rule: When HIGH arrives, it instantly preempts any currently running LOW job regardless of checkpoint status.
- Checkpoint Rule: LOW jobs accumulate runtime in 1-minute increments; a checkpoint is recorded every time total runtime reaches an even number (2, 4, etc.).
- Waste Rule: Upon preemption or completion, if the current minute count is odd, the partial minute accumulated since the last even checkpoint is wasted and reset to 0.
- Resume Rule: After HIGH finishes, the scheduler picks the lowest-priority job from the queue based on start minute (lowest first); if multiple exist, priority among them follows their original arrival order/resumption status logic defined by "start minute wins".
- Completion Order Rule: Jobs are recorded as finished in strict chronological order of their finish events.
