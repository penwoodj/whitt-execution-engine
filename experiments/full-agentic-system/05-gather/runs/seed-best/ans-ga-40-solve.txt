1. **Start Rule**: Jobs (LOW1, LOW2) start at their designated absolute minutes if core is free.
2. **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrival instantly preempts any running LOW job; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3. **Checkpoint/Bank Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of *running* time (absolute minutes: Start+2, then +2 for each subsequent minute run).
4. **Waste Rule**: On preemption, minutes past the last bank are wasted and redone after resume.
5. **Resume Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW outranks a fresh LOW at any tie; core never idles while a LOW waits.
6. **Completion Order Rule**: Lists LOW jobs by finish minute (HIGH excluded).
