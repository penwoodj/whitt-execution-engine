3.  **Wait Rule**: A `LOW` whose start minute arrives while the core is busy simply waits until the core becomes free.
4.  **No-Idle Rule**: The core never idles while a `LOW` job is waiting; the next waiting `LOW` starts in the same minute one previous `LOW` finishes or is preempted.
5.  **Checkpoint Bank Rule**: A job that starts or resumes at absolute minute $s$ banks a checkpoint at absolute minute $s+2$ and again every 2 running minutes after (tracked in absolute minutes).
6.  **Waste/Redo Rule**: On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume; owed time = total duration - banked time.
