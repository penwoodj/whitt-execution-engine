- LOW1 runs 4 minutes starting at minute 0
- LOW2 runs 3 minutes starting at minute 3
- HIGH arrives at minute 3 and runs 6 minutes to completion, instantly preempting whatever LOW job is running, and while HIGH runs nothing else runs
- LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes
- A resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie
- A LOW whose start minute arrives while the core is busy simply waits
- The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting
- The minute one LOW job finishes or is preempted, the next waiting LOW starts in that same minute
- So a LOW can be caught mid-run by HIGH even after a single minute
- LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time
- On preemption the work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume
- A job that starts or resumes at minute s banks a checkpoint at minute s+2 and again every 2 running minutes after, so track banks in absolute minutes
- A bank landing on the exact minute HIGH arrives still counts and nothing is wasted
- After resume a job redoes every minute past its last bank
- So a job with 5 total minutes and 2 banked resumes with 3 minutes left
- Makespan counts from the last finish
- Completion order lists the LOW jobs by the minute each finishes
- A LOW that finishes before HIGH even arrives is always first in the order
