**Phase 1 RULE SCAN**
- **Step Limit Rule**: Steps 1–5 allow up to 2.0% error; Steps 6–10 allow up to 1.0% error.
- **Stage Completion Rule**: A clean observation at position $N$ completes Stage $N$.
- **Rollout Update Rule**: After a stage is completed, rollout increases by 10% $\times$ Stage Number.
- **Breach Penalty Rule**: A breaching observation resets the current step's limit to 0.8%, leaves stages and rollout at the last *completed* values, and marks that line as a breach.
- **Second Breach Stop Rule**: The second breach stops execution immediately with status `STOPPED`.
- **Single Breach Pause Rule**: If exactly one breach occurs and the list ends (no more observations), status becomes `PAUSED`.
- **Clean Finish Rule**: If no breaches occur, status becomes `DONE` upon finishing the last observation.
