### Phase 1 RULE SCAN

Based on the task description and the specific "Canary Discipline" and "Expiry Discipline" instructions provided in the prompt (which override general narrative text), here are the active rules for tonight's event list:

1.  **Limit Rule**: Steps 1–5 have a limit of `2.0`%; Steps 6–10 have a limit of `1.0`%.
2.  **Tightening Rule**: After any rollback, the limit tightens to `0.8`% for every remaining step in the climb.
3.  **Breach Definition**: A breach occurs if `observed > limit`.
4.  **First Breach Action (Rollback)**: On the first breach, pause the climb and roll back exactly one step (`rollback_one`). The current observation is marked `spent` (never retested). The climb resumes from the next observation.
5.  **Second Breach Action (Stop)**: On a second breach, stop everything immediately. Status becomes `STOPPED`. Rollout percentage equals `10 × the step being defended`. Stages done equals observations consumed so far.
6.  **No Breach Completion**: If no breaches occur through Step 10, status is `DONE`, rollout_pct = `10 × steps` (10), stages_done = `steps` (10).

*Note: The "Expiry Discipline" mentioned in the prompt text regarding grants returning half amounts applies to a different contract type ("grant expiry") and does not apply to this specific "canary rollout" task which uses observations, limits, and rollbacks. Therefore, no expiry rules are active for tonight's events.*
