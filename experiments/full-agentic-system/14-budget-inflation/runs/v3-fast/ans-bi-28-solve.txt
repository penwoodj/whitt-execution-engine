**Fact Bullets (Tonight's Numbers Only):**
*   **LOW1**: Duration = 4 minutes, Start = minute 0.
*   **LOW2**: Duration = 3 minutes, Start = minute 3.
*   **HIGH**: Arrival = instant (minute 0), Duration = 6 minutes.

**Rule Application Logic:**
1.  **Arrival Order**: LOW1 arrives at t=0. LOW2 arrives at t=3. HIGH is present at t=0 with preemption priority.
2.  **Preemption Priority**: At any moment, if a job is running and a higher-priority job (HIGH) becomes ready or needs to run, the current job is preempted. Since HIGH arrives instantly and has highest priority, it runs immediately upon arrival of LOW1.
3.  **Checkpoint/Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start + 2` and every subsequent even running minutes relative to its start/resume.
    *   For LOW1 (Start=0): Banks at t=2, t=4...
    *   For LOW2 (Start=3): Banks at t=5, t=7...
4.  **Wasted Calculation**: Minutes run past the last bank are wasted and must be redone after resume. `Owed = Total Duration - Banked`.
5.  **Resume Priority**: A resumed job outranks a fresh one.
