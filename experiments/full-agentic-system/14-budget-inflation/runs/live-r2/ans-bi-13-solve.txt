**Phase 1 RULE SCAN**
1. **Step Limit Rule**: For steps 1-5, max error rate is 2.0%; for steps 6-10, max error rate is 1.0%. Trigger: Arrival of step observation.
2. **Breach Pause & Rollback Rule**: If observed error > current limit, the climb pauses and rolls back to the previous step (N-1). The breaching observation becomes spent. Trigger: Breach condition met at step N.
3. **Tightened Limit Rule**: After any rollback, the limit for all remaining steps tightens to 0.8%. Trigger: Completion of a rollback event.
4. **Stop Condition Rule**: A second breach (total count = 2) stops everything permanently; status becomes STOPPED. Trigger: Second breach condition met.
5. **Success Condition Rule**: If no breaches occur, the climb finishes with status DONE. Trigger: Completion of final step without breach.
