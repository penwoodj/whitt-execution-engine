### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
1. **Step Limit Rule**: Steps 1-5 allow up to 2.0% error; Steps 6-10 allow up to 1.0% error. Triggered by every observation arrival.
2. **Rollback Rule**: Upon first breach, pause climb and rollback exactly one step (returning the partial grant). Triggered by a breach event.
3. **Tightened Limit Rule**: After any rollback, the limit for all remaining steps becomes 0.8%. Triggered by a rollback event.
4. **Stop Rule**: Upon second breach, stop everything immediately. Triggered by a breach event occurring after a previous rollback.
5. **Expiry Return Rule**: When a step is rolled back (trigger request completes), the expired grant returns half of what was GRANTED (the partial amount actually given) rounded DOWN. Triggered by a rollback event.
