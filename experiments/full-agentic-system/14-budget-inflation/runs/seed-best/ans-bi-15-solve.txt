2.  **Breach & Rollback:** If an event exceeds the limit for its step:
    *   The rollout pauses (rollback occurs).
    *   The current step is effectively "failed" and spent.
    *   The climb resumes from the next observation (next step).
3.  **Tightened Limits:** After any rollback, the limit tightens to 0.8% for every *remaining* step. This applies regardless of whether the remaining steps were originally in the 1-5 or 6-10 group. The "tighter" rule overrides the initial grouping rules for subsequent steps.
4.  **Second Breach:** A second breach (exceeding the current active limit) stops everything entirely (`status`: `STOPPED`). The rollout holds at the step where it stopped.
5.  **Success:** If no breaches occur, the rollout completes all steps (`status`: `DONE`).
