### Phase 1: Rule Scan
**Rules identified that change state during the events:**
1.  **Arrival Order Rule**: Determines which LOW job starts next based on absolute start minute (fresh vs resumed), with ties broken by resume status (resumed outranks fresh).
2.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` and every subsequent even number of running minutes thereafter, using absolute time to track the banked state.
3.  **Preemption Loss Rule**: If HIGH preempts a LOW, any running minute past the last valid bank is wasted; on resume, those minutes are owed and redone.
4.  **Completion Order Rule**: LOW jobs are listed by their finish minute (absolute time), ignoring the fact that one may have been preempted earlier.

**Triggers:**
*   Arrival of LOW1 (Minute 0) triggers initial banking sequence.
*   Arrival of LOW2 (Minute 3) enters queue, subject to arrival order rule if core is free or preemption occurs.
*   Arrival of HIGH (Minute 5) triggers the Preemption Loss Rule for whichever LOW holds the core.
