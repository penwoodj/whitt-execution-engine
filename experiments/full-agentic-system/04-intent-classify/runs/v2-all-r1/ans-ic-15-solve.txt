### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Listing every rule that changes state during the event or claim list, including source-ranking logic and verification status.*

1.  **Source Ranking Rule**: `machine_log` > `signed_handoff` > `hallway_hearsay`. When sources agree on a fact, the claim is `true`.
2.  **Contradiction Rule**: When a higher-ranked source contradicts a lower-ranked source regarding a specific fact, the higher source's assertion determines the truth value (claim becomes `false` if the high source says it didn't happen), and the lower source's assertion counts as `FALSE`.
3.  **Hearsay/Dashboard Alone Rule**: A claim resting *only* on `hallway_hearsay` or *only* on the `status_dashboard` is marked `unverified`, regardless of whether the single source says it happened.
4.  **Dashboard Bug Rule**: The `status_dashboard` is a `KNOWN-BUGGY` source and can never carry a claim alone (reinforces Rule 3).
