### Phase 1: Rule List & Event Log

**Rules:**
1. **Arrival Order:** LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes (Lower minute wins).
2. **Resume Priority:** A resumed LOW job outranks a fresh LOW job at any tie.
3. **Non-Idle Core:** The core never idles while a LOW is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts immediately upon preemption/finish.
4. **Checkpoint Banking:** Checkpoints bank at `Start + Resume` time + 2, and every 2 running minutes after (absolute minutes).
5. **Preemption Waste:** Minutes past the last valid bank are wasted on preemption and redone on resume.

**Events:**
1. **Minute 0:** Arrive `LOW1` (Total: 4 min).
2. **Minute 3:** Arrive `LOW2` (Total: 3 min).
3. **Minute 5:** Arrive `HIGH` (Duration: 4 min, Preempt current job, Finish at Minute 9).
