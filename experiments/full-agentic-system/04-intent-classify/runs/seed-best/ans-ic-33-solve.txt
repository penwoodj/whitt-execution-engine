### Phase 1: Rule List
Based on the task description, here are the rules that govern state changes during the event list (arrivals, grants/preemptions, expiries/checkpoints):

1.  **Arrival Order Rule**: Jobs arrive in strict order of their start minutes ($t_{start}$). A resumed job outranks a fresh job at any tie.
2.  **Preemption Rule**: When `HIGH` arrives (at minute 2), it instantly preempts whatever `LOW` job is running. While `HIGH` runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: Checkpoints bank at $t_{start} + 2$ and every subsequent even number of *running* minutes relative to the start/resume time (absolute minutes). A bank landing exactly on the preemption minute counts; no work is wasted.
4.  **Waste Calculation Rule**: On preemption, only minutes past the last valid checkpoint are wasted. The job must redo this work upon resume ($owed = total - banked$).
5.  **Core Utilization Rule**: The core never idles while a `LOW` job is waiting. As soon as one finishes or is preempted, the next waiting `LOW` starts in that same minute.
