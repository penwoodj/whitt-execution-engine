### Phase 1: Rule List & Event Triggers

Based on the task description, here are the rules that change state during the event or claim list (arrivals, grants/preemptions/expiries), one line each:

1.  **Arrival Order Rule**: LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes; a resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie.
2.  **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrives and instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running (if any); while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` and every 2 running minutes after; absolute minute tracking applies.
4.  **Waste/Resume Rule**: On preemption, work past the last bank is wasted (minutes run = total - banked); on resume, the job redoes the owed minutes.
5.  **Idle Avoidance Rule**: The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.

**Event Triggers:**
*   `LOW1` Arrival (Minute 0) -> Triggers Rule 1 (Queueing).
*   `LOW2` Arrival (Minute 3) -> Triggers Rule 1 (Queueing behind LOW1 due to start time tie-breaker logic, though LOW1 is already running).
*   `HIGH` Arrival (Minute 3) -> Triggers Rule 2 (Preemption of LOW1) and Rule 4 (Waste calculation for LOW1).
*   `LOW1` Resume (Minute 6) -> Triggers Rule 4 (Redo owed minutes) and Rule 3 (New banking sequence).
*   `LOW1` Finish (Minute 9) -> Triggers Rule 5 (Core moves to next waiting job, LOW2).
*   `LOW2` Finish (Minute 12) -> End of simulation.
