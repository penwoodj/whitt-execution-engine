### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Listing every rule that changes state during the event or claim list, including source-ranking logic and verification status.*

1.  **Source Ranking Rule**: `machine_log` > `signed_handoff` > `hallway_hearsay` > `status_dashboard`. When sources agree on a fact, the claim is `true`.
2.  **Contradiction Rule**: When a higher source contradicts a lower one regarding a specific event/time/fact, the higher source wins (claim becomes `false`), and the lower source's assertion counts as `false`.
3.  **Hearsay/Dashboard Alone Rule**: A claim resting *only* on hearsay or *only* on the buggy dashboard is `unverified`, not false.
4.  **Verification State Rule**: Claims are binary states (`true`/`false`) if supported by valid sources, or ternary (`true`/`false`/`unverified`) if unsupported by valid sources alone. No partial grants or expiry logic applies to these claims; they are static assertions evaluated at the moment of audit.
