**Phase 1: RULE SCAN**
The following rules govern the state changes during this event sequence (Low Job arrivals/checkpoints, High Job arrival/preemption):
1.  **Arrival Rule**: A Low job enters the waiting queue upon its start minute arriving; `owed` is calculated as `total_duration - banked`.
2.  **Checkpoint/Bank Rule**: Banks accumulate at `start + 2` and every subsequent even number of running minutes (`s+4`, `s+6`...); a bank landing exactly on the High arrival minute prevents waste.
3.  **Preemption/Waste Rule**: When High arrives, if Low is running (or just finished), any time past the last valid bank is marked as `wasted` and must be redone upon resume.
4.  **Resumption Priority Rule**: A resumed Low job outranks a fresh Low job at any tie in start minutes for core selection.
5.  **Non-Idle Core Rule**: The core runs a waiting Low job immediately after a previous one finishes or is preempted within the same minute.
