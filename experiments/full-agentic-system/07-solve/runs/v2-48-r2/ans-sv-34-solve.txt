To solve this task, we must strictly follow the event-driven simulation rules provided: arrivals, preemptions, checkpoint banks, and state transitions. We will walk through every minute of the night to determine the completion order, wasted minutes, and makespan.

### **Phase 1: Rule & Event Analysis**
The events are defined in the "TASK" section:
1.  **LOW1 Arrival**: Minute 0. Total duration: 5 minutes. Priority: Start Minute 0.
2.  **LOW2 Arrival**: Minute 1. Total duration: 2 minutes. Priority: Start Minute 1.
3.  **HIGH Arrival**: Minute 4. Duration: 4 minutes (until completion at Minute 8). Preempts current LOW job immediately upon arrival.

**State Mechanics:**
*   **Core Allocation**: Core is held by the highest priority waiting job. If no jobs are waiting and core finishes, next starts immediately in that minute.
*   **Priority**: Resumed job > Fresh job (at same start minute). Priority determined by Start Minute if both fresh.
*   **Checkpoints**: Banked at `Start/Resume + 2` and every `+2` running minutes thereafter.
    *   If preemption happens at minute $t$, and the last bank was at $b$, then $wasted = (t - b)$.
    *   Upon resume, the job owes `(Total - Banked)` more time.
