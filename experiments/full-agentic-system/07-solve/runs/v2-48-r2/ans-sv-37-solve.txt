### Phase 1: Rule List (State Changes)
Based on the task description and events (LOW1 start, LOW2 start, HIGH arrival/preempt), here are the rules that change state during these events or claims:

1.  **Arrival Order**: Jobs enter the waiting pool in strict order of their requested start time; at any tie, a resumed job outranks a fresh one.
2.  **Start Minute Assignment**: A LOW job takes the core based on its absolute start minute ($s$); if multiple wait, the earliest $s$ goes first.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking**: Every LOW job banks a checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes: $s+2$, then $+4$, etc.).
4.  **Preemption Waste Calculation**: Upon HIGH arrival/preemption, any running minutes past the last banked checkpoint are wasted and must be redone upon resume ($owed = total - banked$).
5.  **Core Continuity**: The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting job starts in the exact minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
6.  **Job Completion Definition**: A job finishes when it completes its total required duration after redone minutes (owes nothing).
