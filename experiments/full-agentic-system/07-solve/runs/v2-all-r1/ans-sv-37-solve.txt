### Phase 1: Rule Scan
The following rules govern state changes during the night's events (arrivals, grants, preemptions, expiries). Each rule is triggered by a specific event type.

1.  **Arrival/Grant Rule**: Triggered when `LOW1` or `LOW2` starts at minute 0 and 3 respectively. This adds a job to the waiting queue with a priority rank equal to its start minute (lower is better).
2.  **Resume Priority Rule**: Triggered upon preemption or completion of a job while others wait. A resumed job outranks a fresh job if their effective ranks are tied.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: Triggered at `start` and every 2 running minutes. Banks occur at absolute minute $t$ where $t = \text{start} + 2k$. A bank landing exactly on the HIGH arrival time counts as valid, preventing waste.
4.  **Preemption/Waste Rule**: Triggered when `HIGH` arrives (minute 3). Any running minutes past the last valid checkpoint are marked as "wasted" and must be redone upon resume. The current job is suspended immediately.
5.  **Completion Rule**: Triggered when a job finishes its owed duration. Updates the makespan if this finish time is the latest, and appends to the completion order (if LOW).
