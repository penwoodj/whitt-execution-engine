### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
*Every rule in the task that changes state during the event or claim list:*

1.  **Arrival Rule**: LOW jobs enter the waiting pool ordered by their start minute; a resumed job outranks a fresh one at any tie. (Trigger: LOW job definition/arrival).
2.  **Core Allocation Rule**: The core runs a LOW job if no HIGH is present and the queue is non-empty; otherwise, it runs nothing until an event triggers a change. (Trigger: Time advancement with waiting jobs).
3.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` minutes absolute time, plus every 2 running minutes after that point. (Trigger: Job start or resume).
4.  **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrival instantly preempts the current LOW job if one is running. (Trigger: HIGH arrival event).
5.  **Waste Calculation Rule**: Minutes run past the last banked checkpoint on a LOW job are wasted and must be redone upon resume. (Trigger: Preemption/Completion of HIGH).
6.  **Resume Priority Rule**: When the core becomes free after preemption or completion, it picks the highest priority waiting LOW (resumed > fresh, lower start minute first). (Trigger: Core free event).
