### Phase 1 RULE SCAN
This phase lists every rule in the task that changes state during the event sequence (tonight's events), identifying the trigger for each.

1.  **Per-Attempt Cost Rule**: Triggers on every attempt (failed or successful). Adds `3` tokens to spend.
2.  **Wait Time Rule**: Triggers after a failed attempt. Adds wait seconds (`3`, then `6`) to spend.
3.  **Total Spend Accumulation Rule**: Aggregates costs from Rules 1 and 2. Used to determine if the budget is exceeded.
4.  **Budget Exceeded Check (First Pass)**: Triggers after total spend calculation. If `spend > budget`, triggers the doubling mechanism.
5.  **Budget Doubling Rule**: Triggers only once when a budget exceedance is detected. Doubles the current budget limit for the re-check.
6.  **Budget Exceeded Check (Re-Check)**: Triggers after doubling. Compares original spend against the new doubled budget to determine final suppression status (`suppressed`).
7.  **Final State Calculation Rule**: Computes `budget_after` as `doubled_budget - spend`.
