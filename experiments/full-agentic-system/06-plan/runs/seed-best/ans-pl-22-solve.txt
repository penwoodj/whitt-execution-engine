### Phase 1: RULE SCAN
The following rules govern the state changes during the event stream. Each rule is listed on one line with its trigger condition.

1.  **Load Rule**: Triggered by a `load` request; if the requested model fits in free space, it loads immediately (no eviction).
2.  **Eviction Rule**: Triggered by a `load` request that does not fit; evicts least-recently-touched (LRT) *unpinned* models one-by-one until space is sufficient or only pinned models remain.
3.  **Deferred Load Rule**: Triggered if an eviction sequence exhausts all unpinned models and the requested model still cannot fit; the load is deferred, no further evictions occur.
4.  **Unload Rule**: Triggered by an `unload` request; frees the specified amount of space (no-op if model not resident).
5.  **Touch/Recency Update Rule**: Triggered immediately after any successful Load or Unload; updates the recency order of the involved models to most-recent.
