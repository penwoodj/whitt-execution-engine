The task requires simulating a scheduling process with specific rules for job execution, preemption, checkpointing, and resumption. The input consists of two LOW jobs (LOW1, LOW2) and one HIGH job arriving at minute 4.

**Rules Analysis:**
1.  **Arrival Order:** Jobs are processed based on their start minutes. If a core is free, the waiting job with the earliest start minute runs. A resumed job outranks a fresh one if they have the same start minute (not applicable here as start times differ).
2.  **Preemption:** HIGH arrives at minute 4 and preempts whatever LOW job is running. While HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpointing:** Jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of *running* time.
    *   Banked checkpoints occur at absolute times $s + 2k$ where $s$ is the start/resume minute and $k \ge 0$.
    *   If a job is preempted, work done since the last banked checkpoint is wasted.
4.  **Resumption:** Upon resuming, the job redoes all minutes past its last bank. The remaining time to run = Total Duration - Banked Minutes.
