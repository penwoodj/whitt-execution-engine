### Phase 1: Rule List
Based on the task description, here are the rules that change state during events or claims (arrivals, grants/preemptions, expiries/checkpoints):

1.  **Arrival Order Rule**: LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes; a resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie.
2.  **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrives and instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` and every 2 running minutes after (absolute minutes).
4.  **Waste Calculation Rule**: On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted (`owed = total - banked`).
5.  **Resume Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie; the core never idles while a LOW waits.
