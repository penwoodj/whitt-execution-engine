### Phase 1: Rule List
Based on the task description, here are the rules that govern state changes during events (arrivals, preemptions, completions):

1.  **Arrival/Granting**: A `LOW` job arriving at minute $s$ grants a checkpoint bank at absolute minute $s+2$.
2.  **Preemption Trigger**: When `HIGH` arrives at minute $t$, it instantly preempts the currently running `LOW` job (if any).
3.  **Waste Calculation**: Upon preemption, minutes run past the last valid checkpoint bank are wasted and must be redone upon resume.
4.  **Resume Priority**: A resumed `LOW` job outranks a fresh `LOW` job at any tie in start/resume time.
5.  **Core Utilization**: The core never idles while a `LOW` job is waiting; the next waiting `LOW` starts immediately when the current one finishes or is preempted.
6.  **Checkpoint Banking**: A checkpoint banks every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes).
7.  **Completion Order**: `LOW` jobs are listed by their finish minute, excluding `HIGH`.
