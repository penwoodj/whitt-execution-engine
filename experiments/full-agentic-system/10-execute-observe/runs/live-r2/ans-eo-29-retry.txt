Phase 1 RULE SCAN:
- Preemption Rule: HIGH arriving at minute 2 instantly preempts any running LOW job; no other jobs run while HIGH runs.
- Checkpoint Rule: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes s, s+2, s+4...).
- Wasted Work Rule: On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume.
- Resume Logic: A resumed job redoes every minute past its last bank; if a job has 5 total minutes and 2 banked, it resumes with 3 minutes left.
- Core Utilization Rule: Core never idles while LOW jobs are waiting; next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous finishes or is preempted.
- Completion Order Rule: LOW jobs are listed by finish time; those finishing before HIGH arrives are always first.
