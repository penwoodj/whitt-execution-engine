The task requires simulating a scheduling process with specific rules for job execution, checkpointing, preemption, and resumption. The input consists of two LOW jobs (LOW1, LOW2) and one HIGH job arriving at minute 4.

**Rules Analysis:**
1.  **Arrival Order:** Jobs are processed based on their start minutes. If a core is free, the waiting job with the earliest start minute runs. A resumed job outranks a fresh one if they have the same start minute (not applicable here as start times differ).
2.  **Execution & Preemption:** HIGH arrives at minute 4 and preempts whatever LOW is running. While HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpointing:** Jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of *running* time. On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted.
    *   Checkpoint times are absolute: $Start + 2$, then every subsequent even number of running minutes.
4.  **Resumption:** When a job resumes, it redoes the work since the last checkpoint. The "owed" time is Total - Banked.
