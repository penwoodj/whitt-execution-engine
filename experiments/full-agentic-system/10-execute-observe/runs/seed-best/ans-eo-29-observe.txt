### Phase 1: Rule List
Based on the task description, here are the rules that change state during the event or claim list (arrivals, grants/preemptions, expiries/checkpoints):

1.  **Arrival Order Rule**: LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes; a resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie.
2.  **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrives and instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: A job banks a checkpoint at `start_or_resume + 2` and every 2 running minutes after (absolute minutes).
4.  **Waste/Resume Rule**: On preemption, work past the last bank is wasted; on resume, the job redoes every minute past its last bank (`owed = total - banked`).
5.  **Expiry Return Rule**: When a grant expires (preemption), it returns half of what was GRANTED (the partial amount actually given) rounded DOWN immediately before the next request is processed.
