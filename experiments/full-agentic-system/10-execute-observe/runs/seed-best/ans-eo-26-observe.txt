### Phase 1: Rule List
Based on the task description, here are the rules that govern state changes during events (arrivals, grants, preemptions, expiries):

1.  **Arrival/Grant Rule**: A `LOW` job request at minute $s$ banks a checkpoint at absolute minute $s+2$. If the core is idle, it starts immediately; if busy, it waits in queue ordered by start time (ties broken by "resumed outranks fresh").
2.  **Checkpoint Bank Rule**: Checkpoints bank every 2 completed minutes of *running* time after the initial bank. A job with $T$ total minutes and $B$ banked minutes resumes with $T-B$ owed.
3.  **Preemption/Expiry Rule**: When `HIGH` arrives at minute $H$, it preempts the current `LOW`. Minutes run past the last checkpoint bank are wasted (expired). The return is half of the *granted* partial amount, rounded down, applied immediately before processing the next request.
4.  **Resume Priority Rule**: A resumed `LOW` job outranks a fresh `LOW` job at any tie in start time or queue position.
5.  **Core Utilization Rule**: The core never idles while a `LOW` is waiting; the next waiting `LOW` starts immediately upon preemption or finish of the current one, within the same minute.
