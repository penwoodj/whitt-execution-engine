### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, preemption, checkpointing, and waste calculation for LOW jobs.*

1. **Arrival Order & Queuing**: LOW jobs take the core in strict order of their start minutes; a resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie; if the core is busy when a LOW's start minute arrives, it waits until the current job finishes or is preempted.
2. **Preemption Priority**: HIGH arrival instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running and stops all other activity while HIGH runs.
3. **Checkpoint Banking**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time; banks occur at absolute minute $s+2$ and every subsequent even interval relative to the start/resume point ($s, s+2, s+4...$).
4. **Waste on Preemption**: On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume.
5. **Resume Calculation**: A job resumes with remaining time = Total Time - Banked Minutes; it redoes every minute past its last bank.
