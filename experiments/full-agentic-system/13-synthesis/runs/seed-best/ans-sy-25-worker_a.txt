2.  **Preemption:** HIGH arrival instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running and stops all other execution while it runs.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking:** A job banks a checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes).
4.  **Waste on Preemption:** On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone after resume.
5.  **No Idle Core:** The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
