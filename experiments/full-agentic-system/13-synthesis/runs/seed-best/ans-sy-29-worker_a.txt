### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, checkpointing, and preemption logic for LOW jobs.*

1.  **Arrival Order:** LOW jobs are queued in order of their start minutes; a resumed job outranks a fresh one at any tie.
2.  **Core Utilization:** The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts immediately upon preemption or completion of the current job within the same minute.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking:** A job banks a checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes $s, s+2, s+4...$).
4.  **Preemption Waste:** On preemption by HIGH, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume.
5.  **Resume Logic:** A resumed job owes the total duration minus the time already banked; it runs from its current minute until completion.
