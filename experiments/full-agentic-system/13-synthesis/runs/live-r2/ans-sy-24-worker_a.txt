### Phase 1 RULE SCAN (First Half Only)
The following rules govern the state changes for the LOW/HIGH job scheduling event:
1. **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrival instantly preempts any running LOW job; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
2. **Core Allocation Rule**: The core is assigned to waiting LOW jobs in order of their start minutes (earliest start first).
3. **Resume Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW job outranks a fresh LOW job at any tie in start minute availability.
4. **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time; banks are tracked in absolute minutes ($s, s+2, \dots$).
5. **Waste Rule**: On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume.
6. **Idle Prevention Rule**: The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
