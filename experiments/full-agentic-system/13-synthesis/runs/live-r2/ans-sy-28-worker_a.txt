### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, preemption, checkpointing, and waste calculation for LOW jobs.*

1. **Arrival Order & Queuing**: LOW jobs are queued in order of their start minutes; a resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie; if the core is busy when a LOW's start minute arrives, it waits until the current job finishes or is preempted.
2. **Preemption Priority**: HIGH arrival instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3. **Checkpoint Banking**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes $s+2$, then every 2 subsequent minutes).
4. **Waste on Preemption**: On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and must be redone after resume.
5. **Resume Calculation**: A job resuming owes the difference between its total duration and its banked time; it runs this owed amount immediately upon core availability.
