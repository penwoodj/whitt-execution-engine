### Phase 1: Rule Scan (Part B - Residency)

**Rules Identified for Part B:**
1.  **Capacity & Eviction Policy**: A single 8GB card with models A(2), B(1), C(4), D(3). Model B is pinned and never evicted. Evictions target the Least-Recently-Touched (LRU) unpinned model.
2.  **Timing Costs**: Loads cost 30s, Unloads cost 10s.
3.  **Load Execution Logic**: A load executes if capacity allows. If a requested item cannot fit, it evicts LRU unpinned models until room is made or only pinned models remain (defer).
4.  **Counting Definitions**:
    *   `loads`: Counts every load command actually executed in the stream.
    *   `evictions`: Counts every eviction actually performed during a load attempt.
    *   `deferrals`: Counts loads that could not fit even after evicting all unpinned models (deferred).
5.  **Deferred ID**: The ID of a deferred load is its own command identifier.
