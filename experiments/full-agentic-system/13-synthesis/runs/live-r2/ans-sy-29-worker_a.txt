### Phase 1 RULE SCAN (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, checkpointing, and preemption logic for LOW jobs.*

1. **Preemption Rule**: `HIGH` arrival instantly stops any running `LOW` job; while `HIGH` runs, no other work executes.
2. **Queue Discipline Rule**: `LOW` jobs are selected from the waiting queue strictly by their absolute start minute (earliest first); at a tie in finish time, a resumed job outranks a fresh one.
3. **Idle Prevention Rule**: The core never idles while a `LOW` job is waiting; the next waiting `LOW` starts immediately upon preemption or completion of the current job within the same minute.
4. **Checkpointing Rule**: A `LOW` job checkpoints every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes $s+2, s+4, \dots$).
5. **Waste Rule**: Upon preemption, any work past the last checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume.
6. **Resume Logic Rule**: A resumed job retains only banked checkpoints; it owes the difference between total duration and banked time to be re-run.
