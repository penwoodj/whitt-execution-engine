### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)

Based on the "night desk sy-22" task description for the first crew, here are the rules governing state changes during the event list:

1.  **Arrival Order:** LOW jobs take the core in strict order of their start minutes ($t_{start}$).
2.  **Resume Priority:** A resumed LOW job outranks a fresh (new) LOW job at any tie in arrival time.
3.  **Wait Condition:** A LOW job whose start minute arrives while the core is busy simply waits until the core becomes free.
4.  **No Idle Core:** The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts immediately when the current one finishes or is preempted.
5.  **Preemption Rule:** HIGH arrival instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running and stops all other execution until HIGH completes.
6.  **Checkpoint Banking:** A job banks a checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes $s, s+2, s+4...$).
7.  **Wasted Work on Preemption:** On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone after resume.
8.  **Resume Calculation:** After resume, a job redoes every minute past its last bank (e.g., if total=5, banked=2, remaining to run = 3).
