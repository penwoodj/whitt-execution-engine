### Phase 1: Rule Scan (First Half Only)
**Rules governing the "night desk sy-22" task:**
1.  **Arrival Order & Preemption:** `HIGH` arrives at minute 4 and runs for 4 minutes, instantly preempting any running `LOW` job. While `HIGH` runs, nothing else runs.
2.  **Core Allocation (Waiting Queue):** `LOW` jobs take the core in order of their start minutes (`LOW1` @ min 0, `LOW2` @ min 1). A resumed `LOW` outranks a fresh one at any tie. If the core is busy when a `LOW`'s start minute arrives, it waits.
3.  **No Idle Core:** The core never idles while a `LOW` job is waiting. When a `LOW` finishes or is preempted, the next waiting `LOW` starts in that same minute.
4.  **Checkpointing & Waste:** `LOW` jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time. On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume. A job banks a checkpoint at `start + 2` and every subsequent even interval relative to its own timeline (absolute minutes).
5.  **Resume Logic:** After resume, a job redoes every minute past its last banked checkpoint.
