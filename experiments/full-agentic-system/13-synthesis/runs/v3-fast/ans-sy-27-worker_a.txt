### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, preemption, checkpointing, and state transitions for LOW jobs.*

1.  **Arrival Order:** LOW jobs are queued in order of their start minutes; a resumed job outranks a fresh one at any tie.
2.  **Preemption Rule:** HIGH arrival instantly preempts whatever LOW is running; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3.  **Checkpoint Banking:** A job banks a checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes $s+2$, then every 2 mins after).
4.  **Waste on Preemption:** Work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone upon resume.
5.  **Resume State:** Upon resume, a job owes all unbanked minutes since its last bank; it resumes with zero progress in those unbanked minutes.
6.  **Core Utilization:** The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts immediately upon preemption or finish of the current one.
