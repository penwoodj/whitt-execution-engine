### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)

**Rules Identified:**
1.  **Arrival Order & Preemption:** `HIGH` arrives at minute 3 and runs for 8 minutes. Upon arrival, it instantly preempts any running `LOW` job. While `HIGH` is active, no other jobs run.
2.  **Job Scheduling (Tie-Breaking):** `LOW` jobs take the core in order of their start minutes (`LOW1` at min 0 vs `LOW2` at min 2). A resumed `LOW` outranks a fresh one at any tie. If the core is busy when a `LOW`'s start minute arrives, it waits until the current job finishes or is preempted in that same minute.
3.  **Checkpointing & Wasted Work:** `LOW` jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time. On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume. A job starting/resuming at minute $s$ banks a checkpoint at $s+2$ and every subsequent even interval relative to its own timeline (absolute minutes).
4.  **Specific Job Durations:** `LOW1` runs for 5 total minutes. `LOW2` runs for 7 total minutes.
