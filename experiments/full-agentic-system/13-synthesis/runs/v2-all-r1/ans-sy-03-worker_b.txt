### Phase 1 RULE SCAN (Second Half Only)
*Rules governing the retry logic for the flaky upstream.*

1.  **Wait Rule**: Initial wait is 2s; each subsequent wait doubles after a failed attempt.
2.  **Cost Rule**: Every attempt costs 2 tokens; every wait second costs 1 token.
3.  **Budget Rule**: Attempt budget starts at 36 tokens. If spend exceeds budget, the job is suppressed and the budget doubles exactly once for re-checking.
4.  **Suppression Rule**: The job is only suppressed if it remains over the *doubled* budget after the re-check; otherwise, it proceeds (implied success on the 4th attempt).
5.  **Trigger Sequence**: Waits occur strictly between failed attempts and before the next run.
