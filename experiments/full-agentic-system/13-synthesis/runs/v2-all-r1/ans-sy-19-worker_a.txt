### Phase 1: Rule Scan (First Half Only)

Based on the task description for the "canary rollout" (Part A), here are the rules governing state changes during the event list:

1.  **Stage Assignment Rule**: The $N$-th observation in the list carries Stage $N$.
2.  **Clean Observation Rule**: If an observation is clean (rate $\le$ limit), it completes the stage equal to its position ($N$). The rollout updates to $10 \times N$.
3.  **Breach Observation Rule**: If an observation breaches (rate $>$ limit), it is spent (completes nothing). State holds at last completed values. Limit tightens to $0.8\%$ for remaining steps.
4.  **Stop Condition Rule**: A second breach stops everything with status `STOPPED`.
5.  **Pause Condition Rule**: Exactly one breach and the list ends results in status `PAUSED`.
6.  **Done Condition Rule**: No breaches at all and the climb finishes results in status `DONE`.
