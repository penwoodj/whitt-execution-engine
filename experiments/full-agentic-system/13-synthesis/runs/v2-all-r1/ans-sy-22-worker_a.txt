### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, preemption, checkpointing, and job ordering.*

1.  **Arrival Order:** LOW jobs take the core in strict order of their start minutes ($s$).
2.  **Resume Priority:** A resumed LOW job outranks a fresh LOW job at any tie (same minute).
3.  **Wait Condition:** A LOW job whose start minute arrives while the core is busy simply waits until the core becomes free.
4.  **No Idle Time:** The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
5.  **Preemption Rule:** HIGH arrival instantly preempts whatever LOW job is running and stops all other execution until HIGH completes.
6.  **Checkpoint Interval:** LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time.
7.  **Wasted Work on Preemption:** On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and must be redone after resume.
8.  **Banking Rule:** A job starting/resuming at minute $s$ banks a checkpoint at $s+2$ and every subsequent even interval of running time (absolute minutes).
9.  **Resume Calculation:** After resume, a job redoes every minute past its last bank; remainder = Total Duration - Banked Minutes.
