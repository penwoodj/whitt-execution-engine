### Phase 1 RULE SCAN (First Half Only)
*Rules governing the core scheduling, checkpointing, and preemption logic for LOW jobs.*

1. **Arrival Order Rule**: The core accepts LOW jobs strictly in order of their start minute; a resumed job outranks a fresh one at any tie.
2. **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrival instantly preempt whatever LOW job is running; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
3. **Checkpoint Bank Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time (absolute minutes $s+2$, then every 2 mins after).
4. **Waste on Preemption Rule**: On preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume.
5. **No Idle Core Rule**: The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
