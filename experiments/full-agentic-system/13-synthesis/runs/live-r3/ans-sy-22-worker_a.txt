### Phase 1 RULE SCAN (First Half Only)
The following rules govern the state changes for the LOW/HIGH job scheduling event:
1. **Preemption Rule**: HIGH arrival instantly preempts any running LOW job; while HIGH runs, nothing else runs.
2. **Queueing Rule**: LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes (earlier start minute = higher priority).
3. **Resume Priority Rule**: A resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie (not applicable here as starts are distinct).
4. **Checkpoint Banking Rule**: LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time; banks occur at absolute minutes $s+2, s+4, \dots$.
5. **Waste Rule**: On preemption, work past the last banked checkpoint is wasted and must be redone after resume.
6. **Idle Avoidance Rule**: The core never idles while a LOW job is waiting; the next waiting LOW starts in the same minute the previous one finishes or is preempted.
