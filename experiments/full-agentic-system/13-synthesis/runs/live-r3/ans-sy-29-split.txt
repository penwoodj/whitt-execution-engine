FIRST HALF SCOPE (Preemptive Core Task):

**Rules:**
*   "LOW1 runs 4 minutes starting at minute 0, LOW2 runs 3 minutes starting at minute 3."
*   "HIGH arrives at minute 3 and runs 6 minutes to completion, instantly preempting whatever LOW job is running, and while HIGH runs nothing else runs."
*   "LOW jobs take the core in order of their start minutes, a resumed LOW outranks a fresh one at any tie, and a LOW whose start minute arrives while the core is busy simply waits."
*   "the core never idles while a LOW job is waiting: the minute one LOW job finishes or is preempted, the next waiting LOW starts in that same minute"
*   "LOW jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time; on preemption the work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume."
*   "a job that starts or resumes at minute s banks a checkpoint at minute s+2 and again every 2 running minutes after, so track banks in absolute minutes"
*   "a bank landing on the exact minute HIGH arrives still counts and nothing is wasted; after resume
