### Phase 1 Rule Scan (First Half Only)

Based on the "night desk sy-26" task description for the first crew, here are the rules governing state changes during the event list:

1.  **Arrival Order & Preemption:** `HIGH` arrives at minute 3 and instantly preempts any running `LOW` job; while `HIGH` runs, nothing else runs.
2.  **Core Allocation (Tie-Breaking):** `LOW` jobs take the core in order of their start minutes; a resumed `LOW` outranks a fresh one at any tie.
3.  **Waiting Logic:** A `LOW` whose start minute arrives while the core is busy simply waits until the current job finishes or is preempted.
4.  **Checkpoint Banking:** `LOW` jobs checkpoint every 2 completed minutes of running time; on preemption, work past the last checkpoint is wasted and redone after resume.
5.  **Banking Schedule:** A job starting/resuming at minute $s$ banks a checkpoint at minute $s+2$ and again every 2 running minutes thereafter (tracked in absolute minutes).
6.  **Resume Cost:** After resume, a job redoes every minute past its last bank; e.g., a job with 5 total minutes and 2 banked resumes with 3 minutes left.
