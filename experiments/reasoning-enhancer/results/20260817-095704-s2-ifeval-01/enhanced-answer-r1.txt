- auth: handles user authentication and session management  
- billing: manages subscription plans and payment processing  
- cache: provides fast data retrieval through stored responses  
- search: enables full-text queries across content repositories  
- indexer: processes and stores structured data for efficient access  
- gateway: routes requests to appropriate backend services  
- queue: manages asynchronous task execution and message delivery  
- audit: records system events for compliance and troubleshooting
