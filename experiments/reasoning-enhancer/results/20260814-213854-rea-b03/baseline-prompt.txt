TASK:
A SaaS had 520 paying users in January. It gained 45 new users, lost 78
to churn, then ran a win-back campaign recovering 30. What is the
current user count? At $9 per user per month, what is the new monthly
revenue? Answer both in one short paragraph.

SOURCE MATERIAL:
520 + 45 = 565. 565 - 78 = 487. 487 + 30 = 517 users. Revenue:
517 x $9 = $4653 per month.

Answer the task directly and completely.
