- Audit Lead: Complete a comprehensive inventory of all existing bugs, including metadata, tags, and history, with 100% of records mapped to a standardized schema; success criterion is 95% data completeness verified by cross-referencing with source databases.  
- Dry-run Manager: Execute a full migration of a test subset (20% of total issues) in a non-production environment, ensuring all workflows and user permissions replicate correctly; success criterion is zero data loss and no workflow failures during the dry-run.  
- Execution Lead: Perform the full migration of all bug records into the new tool using scheduled batch jobs with real-time sync validation; success criterion is 100% of historical issues migrated with no missing or duplicated entries, validated by automated checksums.  
- Verification Specialist: Validate data integrity and functionality across all modules (e.g., reporting, filtering, comments) through end-to-end user testing with at least five QA engineers; success criterion is 100% of test cases passed and all functional workflows confirmed in a live sandbox environment.  
- Cutover Owner: Execute the final migration to production with zero downtime, ensuring all users are seamlessly redirected to the new tool within one hour; success as defined by 99.9% of users reporting successful login and access to their assigned bug records within the first 60 minutes post-cutover.
