TASK:
A doc bundle has 15 chapters of 22 pages each plus a 19-page appendix.
It must be proofed in sessions of 9 pages. How many pages total, how
many full sessions, and how many pages in the final partial session?
One short paragraph.

SOURCE MATERIAL:
15 x 22 = 330. 330 + 19 = 349 pages. 349 / 9 = 38 sessions remainder 7
(38 x 9 = 342, 349 - 342 = 7). So 38 full sessions plus a final partial
session of 7 pages.

Answer the task directly and completely.
