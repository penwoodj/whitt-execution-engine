#### **Architecture: How Retained-Signal Works**

Quanta doesn’t store every event. Instead, it ingests telemetry and then retains only the signal that’s actively used—such as queries made during incidents or active monitoring sessions. When a query is made, Quanta returns results from retained data. If no one has queried in weeks, the data is purged after 30 days of inactivity.

This means your cost doesn’t grow with service count—it grows only when you use it. You’re not paying for “data volume.” You’re paying for insight.
