#### **Architecture: How Quanta’s retention-based cost model works**  
Quanta doesn’t store every log or trace it ingests. Instead, it retains signals only when they are actively queried. When a query is made, the system checks whether the signal has been retained and returns results from that subset. If no query occurs in 30 days, the signal is purged—no cost is charged for its storage.  

This means your observability cost grows only as you use it—not as you ingest it. It’s not a volume-based subscription; it’s a usage-based one tied to actual analysis.
