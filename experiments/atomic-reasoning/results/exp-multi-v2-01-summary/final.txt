- Each generated workflow must pass an honest validator that scores 8 predefined, objective criteria on a 50-point scale, with scores below 45 triggering a fix cycle. The "honest validator" is defined as an impartial, transparent assessment tool that evaluates workflows based on clear, publicly documented criteria such as correctness, completeness, safety, efficiency, scalability, clarity, consistency, and adherence to standards. Each criterion is weighted equally and scored by independent reviewers using standardized rubrics.  
- The generator revisits and re-emits affected sections during failed fix cycles, and three consecutive failures prompt escalation to the user.  
- Validator evidence is required for acceptance; no output is accepted based solely on the generator’s claim of completeness.
