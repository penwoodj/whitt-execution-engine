/monthly budget csv analysis at /tmp/opencode/budgetroll. fixture: 12 months of transactions, 3 columns plus change, wait, four columns: date, category from a set of 8, description, amount, negative for spend. bake in: one category with a steady creep upward, one with a single huge spike month, and a sneaky recurring charge that moved categories midway through the year. the script: per-category monthly rollup table, category by month, then the movers analysis, biggest absolute month-over-month change per category flagged with the month, the creep category detected by a stated linear-fit threshold, and the category-switcher found by matching description patterns across the split. deliverables: rollup.csv, movers.md with the three stories told in plain sentences and the numbers next to the claims, and a total spend per month row at the bottom of the rollup. verify the rollup sums: every transaction appears in exactly one cell of the table, the grand total matches the raw sum to the cent, integer cents arithmetic throughout, no floats. state that check's result in the report. standard library, deterministic, paste the three mover stories in chat, one line each.
