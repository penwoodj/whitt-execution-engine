# Tier-B Case 010 — weight log trend + plateau (LIGHT)

simple stats job, /tmp/opencode/weightlog. fixture: a daily weight log csv, 90 days, seeded generator with realistic noise around a slow downward trend, then a 3-week plateau baked in, and two days missing entirely plus one obviously bogus entry like 20 kilos off. the analysis script: clean pass first, flag the bogus outlier by distance from a rolling median, document the missing days policy, interpolate or skip, pick and state. then compute the 7-day rolling average series, overall trend slope in kilos per week, the plateau detector, a window of 14+ days where the rolling average moves less than some stated threshold, find it and report its date range. output trend.md with a small text plot of the rolling average, one character per day, so the plateau is visually obvious, plus the numbers, slope, plateau range, total change. include the cleaning decisions in the report, what was flagged, what was skipped. rerun with a different seed once to show the pipeline survives different data, second plateau or no plateau, report honestly. standard library, seeded, nothing interactive, paste the text plot and the slope in chat at the end.

stats notes so the numbers stay honest: the trend is a simple least-squares slope over daily values, kilograms per week, stated with the units, not a mystery number. plateaus are runs of at least 14 consecutive days where the rolling 7-day mean moves less than 0.3 kilograms total, thresholds in the rules file where they belong. and the weekly summary table uses monday starts, say so, since week boundaries silently shift numbers when nobody declares them. the chart is ascii in the file, one row per week, because i want to open it in a terminal not launch a browser. paste the trend line verdict in chat, plain words, gaining, losing, or flat, with the slope number next to it.
