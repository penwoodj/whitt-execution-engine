i want a content-based duplicate finder exercised at /tmp/opencode/dupfind. make the fixture tree yourself, around 120 files across 4 subfolders, text files with varied sizes, and plant duplicates three ways: exact byte copies with different names, same content different line endings, and near-duplicates that differ by one line, maybe 8 exact copies, 4 line-ending variants, and 5 near-dupes in total. the finder walks the tree, hashes everything, and groups exact duplicates including the line-ending ones after normalization, normalization rule stated in the output. near-dupes get detected by a similarity pass on same-size-ish files, threshold stated. deliverables: dupes.md listing each group with full paths and which copy you'd keep under a stated rule, shortest path wins or oldest mtime, pick one and say it. plus a stats line, files scanned, groups found, wasted bytes reclaimable if the losers are deleted. the near-dupe pairs and the exact-dupe groups must be clearly separated in the report because they mean different things. don't actually delete anything, this is a report job, and end with the stats line pasted in chat. standard library, deterministic, done.
