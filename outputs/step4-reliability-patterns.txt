1. **Health Checks to Monitor System Health**

   - **File Location:** `infra/health_checks.tf`
   - **Specific Issues:**
     - Lack of health checks for critical services.
     - Health checks not configured to send alerts or trigger actions in case of failure.
   - **Remediation Steps:**
     - Implement health checks using tools like AWS CloudWatch, Google Stackdriver Monitoring, or Prometheus.
     - Configure alerts to notify stakeholders when a service goes down.
     - Integrate with monitoring systems to automatically restart services if they fail.
   - **Expected Benefits:**
     - Early detection of issues before they cause downtime.
     - Improved reliability and availability of critical services.

2. **Circuit Breakers and Retry Policies**

   - **File Location:** `infra/circuit_breakers.tf`
   - **Specific Issues:**
     - Lack of circuit breakers or retry policies in service definitions.
     - Services not configured to handle transient failures gracefully.
   - **Remediation Steps:**
     - Implement circuit breakers using tools like AWS CloudWatch Synthetics, Google Stackdriver Monitoring, or Istio.
     - Configure retry policies for API calls and database operations.
     - Use load balancers with built-in failover mechanisms.
   - **Expected Benefits:**
     - Reduced risk of cascading failures due to transient errors.
     - Improved resilience against service outages.

3. **Load Balancing Strategies**

   - **File Location:** `infra/load_balancers.tf`
   - **Specific Issues:**
     - Lack of load balancing configurations for incoming traffic.
     - Inefficient distribution of traffic across backend servers.
   - **Remediation Steps:**
     - Implement load balancers using tools like AWS Elastic Load Balancing, Google Cloud Load Balancing, or NGINX.
     - Configure round-robin, least connections, or IP hash algorithms to distribute traffic evenly.
     - Use health checks and sticky sessions for improved session management.
   - **Expected Benefits:**
     - Balanced distribution of traffic across servers, reducing load on any single server.
     - Improved user experience by minimizing latency.

By addressing these reliability patterns in your infrastructure-as-code files, you can enhance the overall reliability and scalability of your applications.
